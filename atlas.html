Route Layers

Three arcs, one journey.

Outward Arc

Malaysia → India → Nepal → Pakistan → Iran → Turkey

The road opens: borders, repairs, kindness, cold nights, language, and the first long rhythm of Toyotaki.

Northern Arc

Turkey → Caucasus → Europe → Iceland

The journey climbs north: old cities, strange systems, ferry thresholds, glaciers, wind, and the North Atlantic edge.

Homeward Arc

Iceland → Baltic → Russia → Central Asia → China → Southeast Asia → Malaysia

The route home is still unfolding. Some nodes already hold stories; others wait for the road to arrive.
