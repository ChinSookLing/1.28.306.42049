A Kolkata Local Market

Vegetables, sweets and flowers — just ordinary things I saw on the streets of Kolkata.

India Kolkata Local Market Everyday Life Street Life

Travelling is not always about finding famous places.

Sometimes I simply walk around and look at what people are doing in their ordinary daily life.

In Kolkata, I came across a local market with vegetables, sweets and flowers.

Nothing special. Just people selling vegetables.

Then I saw the local sweets. This is the kind of thing that makes walking around a new place interesting.

And flowers.

Vegetables, sweets and flowers. Three very ordinary things.

But this is also India.

I like these small scenes because they are not arranged for tourists. They are simply part of someone's everyday life.
