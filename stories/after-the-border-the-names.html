Russia · Velikiye Luki · 15 June 2024

After the Border, the Names

过了边境以后，看见那些名字

这一天的 Russia，不是物资，而是名字。纪念碑上一排一排的名字，让战争从历史变成别人家的儿子、丈夫和父亲。

This day in Russia was not about supplies. It was about names. Rows of names on memorials turned war from history into someone’s son, husband, and father.

Location
Road toward Velikiye Luki / Russia
Route Node
Russia · Names Beside the Road
Date
15 June 2024

6 月 15 日，我继续在 Russia 的路上。沿路我开始看到战争纪念碑，有些碑上是一排一排的名字。

On 15 June, I continued along the road in Russia. Along the way, I began to see war memorials. Some carried rows and rows of names.

看久了，那些名字不再像一块碑上的字。那是别人家的儿子，别人家的丈夫，别人家的父亲。

After looking at them for a while, the names no longer felt like words on a monument. They were someone’s son, someone’s husband, someone’s father.

我没有能力评判一个国家的全部历史，也不想把这段路写成政治判断。可是我站在那里时，最直接的感觉就是：战争留下来的不是数字，是家庭里少掉的人。

I could not judge the whole history of a country, and I did not want to turn this road into a political statement. But standing there, the feeling was simple: what war leaves behind is not only numbers. It is the missing people inside families.

那天晚上，我在 Velikiye Luki Park Parking 过夜。Toyotaki 停在树荫和阳光之间。刚从边境、汽油、超市和第一餐慢慢回到路上，停车位本身也变成那天的安定点。

That night, I stayed overnight at Velikiye Luki Park Parking. Toyotaki was parked between shade and sunlight. After the border, petrol, supermarkets, and the first meal, the parking place itself became the stable point of that day.
