Back Under Trees in Xichang

西昌月季谷停车场：回到树下

5 August 的 sleeping area，是月季谷停车场，西昌，中国。连续几天 city-centre hotel rhythm 之后，Toyotaki 终于又停回一个有树、有空地、有一点 overland 呼吸感的地方。

The sleeping area on 5 August was Yuejigu Parking Area in Xichang, China. After several days of city-centre hotel rhythm, Toyotaki finally parked again in a place with trees, open space, and a little overland breathing room.

Location
Xichang / China
Route Node

Date

前面的 Langzhong、Chengdu、Hanyuan 节点，都把 China group phase 的酒店节奏固定下来。到了西昌这张照片，画面突然松了一点：天空、树、空地，还有停在树边的 Toyotaki。

The previous Langzhong, Chengdu, and Hanyuan nodes fixed the hotel rhythm of the China group phase. In this Xichang photo, the scene suddenly loosened a little: sky, trees, open ground, and Toyotaki parked beside the trees.

这不是说 group travel 不好，而是身体很诚实。它记得以前一个人旅行时，怎样找树、找风、找可以把车安静停下来的地方。西昌这一晚，像是把那种旧节奏短短还给了我。

This does not mean group travel was bad. It only means the body is honest. It remembered how, during the solo journey, I used to look for trees, wind, and a place where the van could stop quietly. This Xichang night briefly returned that old rhythm to me.
