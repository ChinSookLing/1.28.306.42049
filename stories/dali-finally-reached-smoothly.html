Dali, Finally Reached Smoothly

终于顺利到达大理

9 August，终于顺利到达大理，中国。这一晚的 sleeping area，是大理白日梦客栈停车场。

On 9 August, I finally reached Dali, China smoothly. The sleeping area that night was the parking area of Dali Bairimeng Guesthouse.

After Lijiang, the route continued toward Dali. The previous days had the surprise of an old town, and also Toyotaki’s temporary air-filter repair. In Dali, this stop is fixed first with one simple sentence: finally reached smoothly.

This memory stays first as a Dali arrival anchor. There are not many details yet, but it places Dali inside the China route: from Lijiang’s night old town to Dali’s blue sky, mountains, and parking area.
