China · Du Fu to Xianyang · 28–29 July 2024

Du Fu Before the Hong Kong Visa Run

去香港折返前的杜甫草堂

28 July，在去处理 Hong Kong same-day visa run 之前，我终于来到杜甫草堂。中小学读过的诗人，忽然不再只是课本上的名字，而是现实路线上的一个地方。

On 28 July, before handling the Hong Kong same-day visa run, I finally reached Du Fu Thatched Cottage. A poet I had studied in primary and secondary school was no longer only a name in textbooks, but a real place on the route.

Location
Du Fu Thatched Cottage → Xianyang / China
Route Node
China / Literature Memory and Visa-Run Approach
Date
28–29 July 2024

我小时候读过杜甫。那时候他只是课本里的诗人，考试里的名字，课文里的句子。可是这一天，我站在杜甫草堂前，突然有一种“终于摸到了”的感觉。

I had read Du Fu when I was young. Back then, he was a poet in textbooks, a name in exams, and lines in school lessons. But on this day, standing at Du Fu Thatched Cottage, I felt as if I had finally touched that memory.

Toyotaki 停在杜甫草堂外。对我来说，这种照片很奇妙：一辆来自 Malaysia 的 camper van，停在中国文学记忆的门口。

Toyotaki parked outside Du Fu Thatched Cottage. To me, this kind of photo felt strange and beautiful: a camper van from Malaysia parked at the doorway of a Chinese literary memory.

路上还有另一个很中国的大尺度观察：大型货车可以拉很多车。每次看到这种 car carrier，我都会忍不住比一比，Toyotaki 在旁边显得多小。

There was also another very China-scale observation on the road: large trucks could carry many cars at once. Whenever I saw a car carrier like this, I could not help comparing it with Toyotaki, which looked small beside it.

28 July 和 29 July 的 sleeping area，是边方村停车场，咸阳，中国。我的下一班 flight 是 29 July 10:35am 飞 Hong Kong，同一天 17:50 从 Hong Kong 回来。

The sleeping area on 28 and 29 July was Bianfang Village Parking Area in Xianyang, China. My next flight was to Hong Kong at 10:35 a.m. on 29 July, with a return from Hong Kong at 17:50 on the same day.

预计到达咸阳国际机场 T2 的时间是 1:15am。那一晚不像普通过夜，而像一条行政路线的前夜：我必须暂时离开 group，完成 China visa-free 的折返，然后再回到队伍里。

The projected arrival time at Xi’an Xianyang International Airport T2 was 1:15 a.m. That night did not feel like an ordinary overnight stop. It felt like the night before an administrative route: I had to leave the group temporarily, complete the China visa-free exit and return, then rejoin the team.

所以这篇一半是杜甫，一半是 visa clock。中国这一段很特别：课本里的诗人、大型运输车、停车场夜晚、机场时间表，全都在同一条路上。

So this memory is half Du Fu, half visa clock. This China section was unusual: a poet from schoolbooks, large transport trucks, parking-lot nights, and airport schedules all existed on the same route.
