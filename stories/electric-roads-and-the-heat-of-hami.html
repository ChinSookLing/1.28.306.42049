China · Xinjiang Heat · 20–21 July 2024

Electric Roads and the Heat of Hami

小电驴、酒店夜晚，和哈密的热

进入中国后，旅程开始出现新的 texture：路边很多电动三轮车和小电驴，吃饭靠抖音和互联网找，住哪里也不再只是自己决定。

After entering China, the journey began to take on a new texture: electric three-wheelers and small electric bikes appeared everywhere, meals were found through Douyin and internet searches, and sleeping places were no longer decided only by myself.

Location
Xinjiang / China · Kuitun → Hami
Route Node
China Early Team Phase / Xinjiang Heat and Four Teams
Date
20–21 July 2024

在中国路上，我很快注意到电动三轮车和小电驴很流行。它们不是风景背景，而是中国日常交通的一部分：安静、灵活、贴着街边生活移动。

On the road in China, I quickly noticed how common electric three-wheelers and small electric bikes were. They were not just background scenery; they were part of everyday Chinese mobility: quiet, flexible, and close to street life.

食物也是这样打开的。云南竹筒粽子、不同的小吃，我都想试一点。自驾游没有固定餐食安排，所以只能自己找、自己判断、自己带大家去试。

Food opened in the same way. Yunnan bamboo-tube zongzi and different small dishes — I wanted to try a little of everything. In a self-drive journey there was no fixed meal plan, so we had to search, decide, and try things ourselves.

20 July 的 sleeping area 是 Hui Jia Express Hotel。刚好在新疆这五天，因为当地安保和反恐相关住宿要求，我们必须住酒店。对 overlander 来说，这改变了平常的停车和过夜方式。

The sleeping area on 20 July was Hui Jia Express Hotel. During these five days in Xinjiang, because of local security and anti-terror related accommodation requirements as we experienced them, we had to stay in hotels. For overlanders, this changed the usual way of parking and sleeping.

21 July，我们在火焰山前留影。这是我最喜欢的照片之一，因为它不只是景点照。它代表四队人马在中国路段真正站到同一条线上：不同车辆、不同背景、同一个方向。

On 21 July, we took a photo in front of the Flaming Mountains. It is one of my favourite photos because it is not only a tourist-stop image. It represents the four teams standing on the same China route: different vehicles, different backgrounds, one shared direction.

同一天晚上，sleeping area 是哈密香芝丽酒店，哈密，中国。天气 app 显示 Hami excessive heat，37°C。那时我只希望：过了新疆，天气不要再这么热。

That same night, the sleeping area was 哈密香芝丽酒店 in Hami, China. The weather app showed excessive heat in Hami: 37°C. At that moment, I only hoped that after Xinjiang, the weather would be less hot.

这一篇的中国，不是宏大的中国，而是很具体的中国：街边小电驴、试吃一点点、酒店规定、火焰山高温，还有四队人马开始适应同一条路线。

The China in this memory is not a grand idea of China. It is a very concrete China: small electric bikes on the street, trying a little food, hotel requirements, the heat of the Flaming Mountains, and four teams beginning to adapt to one shared route.
