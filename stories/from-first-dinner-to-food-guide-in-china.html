China · Team Phase Begins · 17–19 July 2024

From First Dinner to Food Guide in China

从第一顿晚餐，到带大家找中国美食

进入中国以后，旅程的节奏变了。前面 266 天，我大多数时候是一个人处理路线、停车、吃饭和问题；现在，我进入了 China team phase，要学习和不同文化背景的人一起跨越中国 5000 km。

After entering China, the rhythm of the journey changed. For the previous 266 days, I had mostly handled the route, parking, food, and problems alone. Now I had entered the China team phase, and had to learn how to cross 5,000 km of China with people from different cultural backgrounds.

Location
China → Kuitun / China
Route Node
China Opens / Team Phase and Food Scout Role
Date
17–19 July 2024

17 July，是进入中国后的第一顿晚餐。那一晚的 sleeping arrangement 是仙桃大酒店。对我来说，这顿饭不是普通晚餐，而是一个新阶段的第一张合照：solo road 结束了，团队路段开始了。

17 July was the first dinner after entering China. That night’s sleeping arrangement was at 仙桃大酒店. To me, this was not just an ordinary dinner. It was the first group photo of a new phase: the solo road had ended, and the team road had begun.

18 July 早上，是第一顿中国早餐。旅程刚进入中国，最先打开的不是景点，而是桌上的热汤、面、早餐店灯箱和中文菜单。

The morning of 18 July was the first breakfast in China. The journey did not first open through a tourist spot, but through hot soup, noodles, breakfast-shop lights, and Chinese menu boards.

第二天，我带大家找到家庭式丸子汤和爆炒牛肉。因为这是自驾游，不是旅行团，没有任何固定食物安排；吃什么、去哪里吃，都要靠抖音和互联网资讯自己找。

On the second day, I led everyone to a homestyle meal of meatball soup and stir-fried beef. Because this was a self-drive journey, not a tour group, there was no fixed food arrangement. What to eat and where to eat had to be found through Douyin and internet searches.

18 July 和 19 July，我们住在 Kuitun 奎屯克拉99酒店。因为同伴需要修理 motor，旅行代理商建议又多给一天，所以原本 26 天的中国路段，变成了 27 天。

On 18 and 19 July, we stayed at Kuitun 奎屯克拉99酒店. Because one companion needed motor repair, the travel agent suggested giving us one more day. So the China section changed from 26 days to 27 days.

第三天，我们吃酸菜鱼。没有人安排餐食，所以我靠抖音和互联网资讯，带其他 foreign overlanders 去尝中国美食。以前我是一个人找吃的；现在，我开始帮一群人找吃的。

By the third day, we ate sour fish. With no arranged meals, I used Douyin and internet searches to lead the other foreign overlanders to local Chinese food. Before, I found food for myself; now I had begun finding food for a group.

所以 China opening 对我来说，不是一个 gate 的照片，而是一张饭桌。它从第一顿晚餐、第一顿早餐、motor repair 多出来的一天、再到我带大家找吃的开始。这里，Tuzi 的角色从 solo driver，慢慢变成 team 的 food scout 和 daily rhythm organizer。

So China did not open to me through a photo of a gate. It opened through a dining table: the first dinner, the first breakfast, an extra day caused by motor repair, and then my role in leading people to food. Here, Tuzi’s role slowly changed from solo driver into the team’s food scout and daily rhythm organizer.
