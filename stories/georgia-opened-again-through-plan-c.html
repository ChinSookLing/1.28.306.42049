Georgia · Camp A Way · 24–25 June 2024

Georgia Opened Again Through Plan C

Georgia 再次打开，是因为 Plan C

24 June，我终于重新进入 Georgia，在 Camp A Way 安顿下来。原本我以为这里会是等待 transit visa 的地方，后来才发现，这里真正给我的不是等待，而是一条完全不同的路。

On 24 June, I finally entered Georgia again and settled at Camp A Way. I first thought this would be the place where I waited for a transit visa. Later, I realised that what this place gave me was not waiting, but an entirely different route.

Location
Camp A Way / Georgia
Route Node
Georgia · Return / Route Rebuild
Date
24–25 June 2024

原本的 Plan A 是：24 June 到 11 July 留在 Georgia，等待从 Russia 到 Kazakhstan 的 transit visa；12 July 到 14 July 处理 transit visa；15 July 到 26 July 再进入 Kazakhstan。

The original Plan A was: stay in Georgia from 24 June to 11 July while waiting for the transit visa from Russia to Kazakhstan; handle the transit visa from 12 to 14 July; then enter Kazakhstan from 15 to 26 July.

可是 25 June，我发现 Plan A 行不通。原因不只一个：我已经约好 17 July 在 Kazakhstan border 跟另外几组人会合，而我也找不到 certified translator 把我的 Malaysia driving licence 翻译成 English。

But on 25 June, I realised that Plan A would not work. There was more than one reason: I had already arranged to meet several other groups at the Kazakhstan border on 17 July, and I could not find a certified translator to translate my Malaysian driving licence into English.

幸运的是，我在 Camp A Way 遇到一些 campers。他们教我另一条路：不要继续等 Plan A，改走 ferry to Baku。这个建议打开了 Plan C。

Luckily, I met other campers at Camp A Way. They taught me another way: do not continue waiting for Plan A; take the ferry route through Baku instead. That suggestion opened Plan C.

Plan C 的意思是：我要用 container 把 campervan 载去 Azerbaijan 的 Baku 港口，大约 4 天；我自己搭飞机去 Baku；然后再用 car ferry 去 Kazakhstan 的 Aktau，大约 4 天；最后从 Aktau 驾大约 3800 km 去 Horgos。

Plan C meant this: send the campervan by container to Baku Port in Azerbaijan, about four days; I would fly to Baku myself; then use the car ferry to Kazakhstan’s Aktau, about four days; and finally drive about 3,800 km from Aktau to Horgos.

这条路不是比较轻松，而是比较可行。Georgia 再次打开，不是因为它让我停下来，而是因为它让我重新设计路线。

This route was not easier, but it was more workable. Georgia opened again not because it allowed me to stop, but because it allowed me to redesign the route.

计划变成：24 June 到 30 June，从 Georgia 处理去 Baku；29 June 我飞去 Baku；30 June 收回 campervan；1 July 到 4 July 去 Aktau；5 July 到 11 July，每天大约 500 km，开 3800 km 去 Horgos。

The new plan became: from 24 to 30 June, arrange the Georgia-to-Baku transfer; fly to Baku on 29 June; receive the campervan on 30 June; travel to Aktau from 1 to 4 July; then drive about 500 km per day, 3,800 km total, toward Horgos from 5 to 11 July.

这一天我明白，路上的计划不是一条直线。Plan A 会失败，Plan C 会出现，而真正重要的是：要在崩掉的计划旁边，找到还可以继续走的路。

That day I understood that plans on the road are not straight lines. Plan A can fail, Plan C can appear, and what matters most is finding a route that can still continue beside the broken plan.
