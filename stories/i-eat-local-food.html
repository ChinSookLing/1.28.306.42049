India · Kolkata · Story 14

I Eat Local Food

I saw MAGGI on the menu and immediately felt familiar.

India Kolkata Local Food MAGGI Everyday Life

I was in Kolkata and wanted to try some local food.

Then I saw something very familiar on the menu: MAGGI.

MAGGI is very familiar to me in Malaysia. So when I saw it here in India, I became curious.

Of course, I had to try it.

It was a small thing, but it made me smile. You travel far away, and sometimes you find something familiar in a completely different place.

Same MAGGI. Different country. Different taste.
