Real Story

The moment I stopped and looked up.

Walking through Kolkata, Tuzi noticed an older local man climbing up to the wiring along the street.

The scene was difficult to ignore. There were wires everywhere, and he was working high above the street with the city continuing around him as if this were simply another ordinary day.

Tuzi did not know exactly what repair or maintenance work he was carrying out. But seeing him up there made her stop and look.

Different places have different ways of keeping a city alive.

In Malaysia, Tuzi was familiar with a much more formal idea of electrical maintenance, with specialised technicians and utility systems such as TNB.

Kolkata showed her another everyday scene. Not a landmark. Not a tourist attraction. Just a person, a ladder, a complicated collection of wires, and a job being done above a normal street.

It was one of those moments that made Tuzi think: OMG.

Travel sometimes teaches through beautiful scenery. Sometimes it teaches by making you look up.

What remained in memory

I do not know his name. I do not know exactly what he was repairing. I only know that I saw him there.

And for a traveller walking through an unfamiliar city, sometimes that is enough for a memory to stay.
