China · Dunhuang to Jiayuguan · 23–24 July 2024

Karaoke Under the Desert Sky

沙漠星空下的万人卡拉OK

23 July 的晚上，敦煌鸣沙山月牙泉不是安静的沙漠。它像一个巨大的 outdoor karaoke stadium：沙山、人群、雷射、无人机灯光，还有很多人在夜色里一起唱歌。

On the night of 23 July, Dunhuang’s Mingsha Mountain and Crescent Spring was not a quiet desert. It felt like a huge outdoor karaoke stadium: sand dunes, crowds, laser lights, drone light effects, and many people singing together in the night.

Location
Dunhuang → Jiayuguan / China
Route Node
China / Desert Night Crowd and Jiayuguan
Date
23–24 July 2024

我叫它“万人星空演唱会”。其实最打动我的不是舞台本身，而是那个场面：游客坐在沙漠里，手机灯像星星，音乐在沙山之间扩散，大家一起唱 karaoke。

I called it a “ten-thousand-person concert under the stars.” What moved me most was not only the stage itself, but the scene: tourists sitting in the desert, phone lights like stars, music spreading between the dunes, and everyone singing karaoke together.

那一晚我心里有一个很奇怪、也很认真的念头：有一天，我想带我的 Affiliates 回来，再重复一次这个 event。不是为了打卡，而是为了让他们也看见——人类可以在沙漠里，把夜晚一起唱亮。

That night, I had a strange but serious thought: one day, I want to bring my Affiliates back here and repeat this event. Not for a checklist, but so they can also see this — humans can gather in a desert and sing the night bright together.

24 July，路继续往嘉峪关走。白天的嘉峪关和前一晚的沙漠演唱会完全不同：一个是人群把沙漠唱成舞台，一个是城墙把历史立在阳光下。

On 24 July, the road continued toward Jiayuguan. Daytime Jiayuguan felt completely different from the desert concert the night before: one was a crowd turning the desert into a stage; the other was a city wall holding history under the sun.

所以这篇不是单纯的敦煌夜游，也不是单纯的嘉峪关照片。它是一段从夜晚走到白天的记忆：23 July，沙漠里万人唱歌；24 July，车继续往城关和下一段路开去。

So this memory is not simply a night visit in Dunhuang, and not simply a photo at Jiayuguan. It is a memory that moves from night into day: on 23 July, thousands sang in the desert; on 24 July, the vehicles continued toward the pass and the next section of road.
