Long-run Mode and the Animal Crossings

长奔模式，与随时过马路的动物们

Kazakhstan 打开以后，真正的长奔 mode 开始了。目标很清楚：从 Kuryk / Aktau 一路往 Horgos 方向跑，8 到 10 天完成大约 3800 km。

After Kazakhstan opened, long-run mode truly began. The goal was clear: from Kuryk / Aktau toward Horgos, about 3,800 km in 8 to 10 days.

Location
Boranqul → Aktobe E38 Rest Area → toward Aral / Kazakhstan
Route Node
Kazakhstan · Long-run Mode / Road-life Animal Crossings
Date
8–10 July 2024

8 July，我自拍的时候，刚好碰上一只骆驼开嘴，好像在笑。长途路上有时候就是这样：你只是想记录一下自己还在路上，结果旁边的动物自己变成主角。

On 8 July, while taking a selfie, I happened to catch a camel with its mouth open, as if it was smiling. On a long road, moments like this happen: you only want to record that you are still moving, and suddenly an animal becomes the main character.

印度有牛魔王；Kazakhstan 不但有牛魔王，还有羊小弟、骡小兵、骆驼大兄、马大哥。它们喜欢什么时候过马路，就什么时候过。长奔 mode 不是只有公里数，也有这些随时出现的 road-life。

In India, there were the “cow kings.” In Kazakhstan, there were not only cows, but also sheep, mules, camels, and horses. They crossed the road whenever they wanted. Long-run mode was not only about kilometres; it also carried these unexpected road-life crossings.

8 July 晚上，我停在 Keruyen Saray Hotel Parking, Boranqul, Kazakhstan。长奔 mode 的第一段不是浪漫的风景，而是找得到一个地方安全停下来。

On the night of 8 July, I stopped at Keruyen Saray Hotel Parking, Boranqul, Kazakhstan. The first part of long-run mode was not romantic scenery; it was finding a safe place to stop.

9 July，我继续往前，overnight 在 Aktobe E38 rest Area。每天的重点都很简单：不要贪心，不要出事，能安全地把 Toyotaki 和自己推进下一段。

On 9 July, I continued forward and overnighted at the Aktobe E38 rest area. The focus each day was simple: do not be greedy, do not get into trouble, and move Toyotaki and myself safely into the next section.

10 July，路继续往 Aral 方向。我在 highway rest stop 过夜。Kazakhstan 的天很大，路很长，夕阳和停车点都变成这段 3800 km 里面的标记。

On 10 July, the road continued toward Aral. I overnighted at a highway rest stop. Kazakhstan’s sky was wide, the road was long, and the sunset and parking places became markers inside this 3,800 km stretch.

这一篇 memory 不是一个景点，而是一种节奏：开、停、等动物过、再开；找停车点、看天色、计算明天还要跑多少公里。Kazakhstan 的长奔 mode，就是这样一段一段推进的。

This memory is not a tourist spot; it is a rhythm: drive, stop, wait for animals to cross, then drive again; find a place to park, watch the sky, and calculate how many kilometres remain for tomorrow. Kazakhstan’s long-run mode moved forward like this, section by section.
