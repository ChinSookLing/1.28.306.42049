Kazakhstan · Kuryk → Aktau · 7 July 2024

Kazakhstan Opened at Kuryk

Kazakhstan 在 Kuryk 打开

从 Alat 到 Kuryk 的 ferry 航程大约 30 个小时，所以我是在 ferry 上过了 5 July 和 6 July 的夜。7 July，Toyotaki 和我终于 touch on Kazakhstan。

The ferry from Alat to Kuryk took about 30 hours, so I spent the nights of 5 July and 6 July on the ferry. On 7 July, Toyotaki and I finally touched Kazakhstan.

Location
Kuryk → Aktau / Kazakhstan
Route Node
Kazakhstan Opens / Long-run Mode Begins
Date
7 July 2024

只要成功到达 Kuryk，Plan C 就真正跨过 Caspian Sea。前面是 3800 km，到 Horgos 的长奔 mode：8 到 10 天的路，只求 15 July 顺利抵达。

Once I reached Kuryk, Plan C had truly crossed the Caspian Sea. Ahead was long-run mode toward Horgos: 3,800 km in 8 to 10 days, aiming to arrive safely by 15 July.

在 Azerbaijan 的 Alat，因为 Armenia 和 Azerbaijan 仍处在敏感的休战关系，我被要求把 Armenia 国旗取下来。来到 Kazakhstan 以后，我才把 Armenia 国旗重新放回 Toyotaki 里面。

In Alat, Azerbaijan, because Armenia and Azerbaijan were still in a sensitive ceasefire relationship, I was asked to remove the Armenia flag. After reaching Kazakhstan, I put the Armenia flag back inside Toyotaki.

那一天我也遇见当地人。旅行不是只有过关、费用、航程和公里数；它也会突然停下来，让你和一个地方的人站在一起。

That day, I also met local people. Travel is not only about borders, costs, sailing hours, and kilometres; sometimes it pauses and lets you stand beside people from a place.

晚上，我停在 Shkola Nomer 5 Mangystau, Aktau, Kazakhstan parking。Toyotaki 又变回我的房间、我的车、我的路。第二天开始，就要真的往 Horgos 方向长奔。

That night, I parked at Shkola Nomer 5 Mangystau, Aktau, Kazakhstan. Toyotaki became my room, my vehicle, and my road again. From the next day, the long run toward Horgos would truly begin.

这一篇 memory 是 Kazakhstan 的第一步：不是大城市入口，而是 Kuryk 的路牌、重新挂回的 Armenia 国旗、当地人的合照，还有一个傍晚停车点。

This memory is Kazakhstan’s first step: not a grand city entrance, but a Kuryk road sign, the Armenia flag returning, a photo with a local person, and an evening parking place.
