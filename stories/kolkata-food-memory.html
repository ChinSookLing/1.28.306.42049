Kolkata Food Memory

Debonair, biryani, and the roadside food challenge.

26/10/2023 · Kolkata

A food day before Toyotaki was released.

Toyotaki was still waiting. Tuzi was walking around Kolkata, discovering the city before the road journey had properly begun.

One of the stops was Debonair Veg Restaurant. The menu itself became part of the memory.

The unforgettable biryani.

Then came the biryani.

It became one of those foods that stayed in Tuzi's memory far beyond Kolkata. The same kind of biryani memory would appear again later in Nepal, Pakistan and Iran.

Then I took the roadside challenge.

People always said: don't eat food by the roadside.

But Tuzi dared the challenge.

After all, if this journey was really about seeing ordinary life, then ordinary food was part of it too.

Why This Memory Stayed

Because food was also a way of entering India.

Kolkata was not only traffic, horns, strangers and waiting for Toyotaki. It was also a restaurant menu, a plate of biryani, and the decision to sit down at a roadside food shop.

The journey had not really started on the road yet. But Tuzi was already learning how to enter a country: by looking, tasting, trusting, and sometimes taking the challenge.
