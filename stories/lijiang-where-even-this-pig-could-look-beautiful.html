Lijiang, Where Even This Pig Could Look Beautiful

丽江：连属猪的我都会被拍得美丽动人

7 August 和 8 August 的 sleeping area，是丽江光碧花园客栈（古城木府店），丽江，中国。280 天了，看了那么多古城、出土遗址、公园、博物馆，但真正让我惊艳的，so far 只有这个：丽江古城。

The sleeping area on 7 and 8 August was Lijiang Guangbi Garden Inn, Old Town Mufu branch, Lijiang, China. After 280 days on the road, after so many old towns, archaeological sites, parks, and museums, only one place truly surprised me so far: Lijiang Old Town.

Lijiang / China

Date

它不是某一个角落漂亮。它是完整的、连贯的、有秩序地美好。夜里的灯、木门、石板路、茶叶、辣椒、蒜头、白萝卜、穿民族服饰拍照的人、店里的猫，全部都像被安排在一个很会拍照的世界里。

It was not beautiful because of one single corner. It felt complete, coherent, and beautifully ordered. The night lights, wooden doors, stone lanes, tea shops, hanging chilies, garlic, white radishes, people taking portraits in ethnic dress, and even the shop cat all seemed to belong inside a world that knew how to be photographed.

我鼓励自己来古城这里吃喝玩乐一下，顺便拍个硬照。前面很多天都在赶路、配合 group rhythm、处理住宿、签证和 Toyotaki 的问题。到了丽江，我终于觉得可以稍微把自己放进画面里。

I encouraged myself to eat, walk, play, and take a proper portrait in the old town. For many days before this, I had been moving with the group rhythm, handling hotels, visa timing, and Toyotaki problems. In Lijiang, I finally felt I could place myself inside the picture.

我看见穿民族服饰拍照的人，也看见帮别人拍照的人。古城像一个巨大的摄影棚，可是它又不是假布景。它有生活、有店、有茶、有猫、有巡逻的人，也有很多跟我一样想把这一晚留下来的人。

I saw people taking photos in ethnic dress, and people photographing them. The old town felt like a huge photo studio, but not a fake set. It had life, shops, tea, cats, police on duty, and many people like me who wanted to keep that night.

所以我那句“连猪都会被拍得美丽动人”，不是在骂谁。猪是我自己——我是属猪的。意思是：连我这个属猪的人，在丽江这样的光线、街道和气氛里，都会被拍得美丽动人。

So when I said “even a pig could look beautiful here,” I was not insulting anyone. The pig was me — I was born in the Year of the Pig. I meant that even this Year-of-the-Pig woman could look beautiful in Lijiang’s light, streets, and atmosphere.

这一晚，丽江不是 checklist。它是 280 天之后，一个真的让我停下来、承认“这个地方很美”的地方。

That night, Lijiang was not a checklist stop. After 280 days, it was a place that truly made me stop and admit: this place is beautiful.
