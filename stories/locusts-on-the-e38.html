Kazakhstan · E38 Highway · 11 July 2024

Locusts on the E38

E38 路上的蝗虫与乡下睡床

11 July，Kazakhstan 的 long-run mode 继续。那一天我看到一大堆昆虫，一开始以为是恐怖的蝉。后来 Kazakhstan 的朋友确认：那是 locusts，蝗虫。

On 11 July, Kazakhstan’s long-run mode continued. That day I saw a large number of insects and first thought they were frightening cicadas. Later, a friend in Kazakhstan confirmed that they were locusts.

Location
On the way to Shymkent / E38 Highway Rest Stop, Kazakhstan
Route Node
Kazakhstan · Long-run Mode / Locusts and Rural Rest Stops
Date

这段不能只用照片保存，因为数量、声音和移动感才是记忆本身。它不是一只虫，而是一整片 road-life 突然出现在 Kazakhstan 的 E38 路上。

This cannot be preserved only as a still photo, because the number, sound, and movement are the memory itself. It was not one insect; it was a whole layer of road-life appearing on the E38 in Kazakhstan.

在 Kazakhstan 的乡下，我也看到一种熟悉的生活方式：像 Iran 和 India 一样，人们可以在外头睡觉。对赶路的人来说，这些 rest stop 不只是地图上的点，也是身体可以暂时放下来的地方。

In rural Kazakhstan, I also saw a familiar way of life: like in Iran and India, people could sleep outside. For someone driving long distances, these rest stops were not only points on the map, but places where the body could pause for a while.

11 July 晚上，我停在往 Shymkent 方向的 E38 highway rest stop。长奔 mode 到这里，已经不是“今天看见什么”，而是“今天能不能安全停下来”。

On the night of 11 July, I stopped at an E38 highway rest stop on the way to Shymkent. By this stage of long-run mode, the question was no longer only “what did I see today,” but “can I stop safely tonight?”

这一篇 memory 是 Kazakhstan 长奔 mode 的另一种 texture：蝗虫、户外睡床、路边商店、停车点。不是所有旅程记忆都是 grand view；有些是虫声、热风和一个可以过夜的地方。

This memory is another texture of Kazakhstan’s long-run mode: locusts, outdoor beds, roadside shops, and parking places. Not every travel memory is a grand view; some are insect sounds, hot wind, and a place where you can spend the night.
