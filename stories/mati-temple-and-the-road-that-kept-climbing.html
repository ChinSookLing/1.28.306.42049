Mati Temple and the Road That Kept Climbing

马蹄寺和继续升高的路

26 July，终于来到顶顶大名的马蹄寺。前几天的中国记忆有饭桌、高温、酒店登记、沙漠 karaoke、张掖打点滴的树；这一天，路开始把我带到更高的空气里。

On 26 July, I finally reached the famous Mati Temple. The China memories of the previous days had been dining tables, heat, hotel registration, desert karaoke, and Zhangye’s trees on IV drips. On this day, the road began carrying me into higher air.

马蹄寺本身很有存在感。Toyotaki 停在寺门前，那种画面很像一张 route stamp：一辆来自 Malaysia 的车，停在中国西北一座有名的寺前。

Mati Temple had a strong presence. With Toyotaki parked in front of the temple gate, the scene felt like a route stamp: a vehicle from Malaysia standing before a famous temple in northwestern China.

那天另一个很重要的信号，是海拔。截图显示 Toyotaki 爬到 3313.7 米。即使这一轮因为 group decision 最后没有去 Tibet，这张截图仍然是中国路段里很重要的 high-altitude road moment。

Another important signal that day was altitude. The screenshot showed Toyotaki climbing to 3,313.7 metres. Even though this round did not go to Tibet because of a group decision, this screenshot remains an important high-altitude road moment in the China section.

这也是 skeleton archive 很需要的节点。以后硬盘里的更多照片和视频回来时，这里可以继续插入：当天的路况、身体反应、天气、队伍节奏、以及 Toyotaki 爬高时的真实状态。

This is also a node the skeleton archive needs. Later, when more photos and videos return from the hard drives, this point can hold more material: the road conditions, body response, weather, team rhythm, and Toyotaki’s real state while climbing higher.

26 July 的 sleeping area 是如家酒店·neo（西宁火车站店），西宁，中国。那一晚，Toyotaki 停在城市酒店外，白天的高海拔路段暂时收进夜色里。

The sleeping area on 26 July was Home Inn Neo, Xining Railway Station branch, Xining, China. That night, Toyotaki parked outside the city hotel, and the day’s high-altitude road folded itself into the night.

这一篇不是“我们后来去了哪里”的铺垫。它只是诚实记录：那一天，我们没有去 Tibet；但路还是继续升高，Toyotaki 也真的爬到了 3313.7 米。

This memory is not a setup for somewhere we later went. It records the day honestly: we did not go to Tibet, but the road still kept climbing, and Toyotaki really reached 3,313.7 metres.
