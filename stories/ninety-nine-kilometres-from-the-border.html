Laos · Nam Phe · 16 August 2024

Ninety-Nine Kilometres from the Border

离边境 99 公里，先停下来

16 August，还剩 99 km 到 border。可是已经 5:30pm，天雨路滑，山路九曲十八弯，路又坑坑洞洞。那一刻，最好的决定不是硬冲，而是马上停下来。

On 16 August, there were still 99 kilometres to the border. But it was already 5:30pm. The road was wet from rain, slippery, winding, and full of potholes. At that moment, the best decision was not to push forward, but to stop immediately.

Nam Phe village / Laos

刚从边境 workshop 的 repair mode 出来，Toyotaki 继续往前走。可是 overland 不是每次都靠勇敢。有些时候，真正保命的不是再开 99 km，而是承认今天的路况不适合继续赌。

After the repair mode at the border workshop, Toyotaki continued onward. But overland travel is not always about bravery. Sometimes what keeps you safe is not driving another 99 kilometres, but admitting that today’s road conditions are not worth gambling on.

这是一条很简单的 decision rule：天色、雨、山路、坑洞、边境距离，加在一起，就是不要硬来。停在 Nam Phe 乡村某人家的庭园里，手里拿着杯子，看着雨，路没有断，只是今天不再继续。

It was a simple decision rule: time of day, rain, mountain bends, potholes, and distance to the border all added up to one answer — do not force it. Stopping in someone’s garden in Nam Phe village, cup in hand, watching the rain, did not mean the road had ended. It only meant the road would not continue today.

这篇不是景点记忆。它是 road judgement 的记忆：知道什么时候前进，也知道什么时候停。

This is not a sightseeing memory. It is a memory of road judgement: knowing when to move, and knowing when to stop.
