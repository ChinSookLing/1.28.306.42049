Azerbaijan · Baku · 3 July 2024

Old Stones, Then the Customs News

老城石头，然后海关消息

3 July，我人在 Baku。Toyotaki 还没有回到我身边，所以早上我先去看这座城市：阿塞拜疆共和国的首都，城市规划有些地方让我觉得和 Malaysia 差不多，可是我住在老城区，身边全是百年老石头。

On 3 July, I was in Baku. Toyotaki had not returned to me yet, so in the morning I first walked into the city: the capital of Azerbaijan, with urban planning that in some places reminded me of Malaysia, while I was staying among the old stones of the old city.

在 Baku 的老城区，我一直感觉这不是普通的 tourist place。这里的石头不是布景，而像是一层一层城市时间留下来的东西。后来我才知道，当今 Baku 地区的人类定居痕迹可以追溯到石器时代；在 Bayil 附近发现过青铜时代的岩石雕刻，老城区也发现过青铜小鱼雕像。

In Baku’s old city, I kept feeling that this was not just a tourist place. The stones did not feel like decoration; they felt like layers of city time. I later learned that traces of human settlement in the Baku region go back to the Stone Age, with Bronze Age rock carvings found near Bayil and a bronze fish figure discovered in the old city.

可是旅行不是只有看城市。下午，我收到一个好消息：Toyotaki 可能快到 Baku Custom 了。于是我马上从原本住的地方 move out，叫 taxi 搬去靠近 customs 的 Black Mount Hotel。

But the journey was not only about seeing the city. In the afternoon, I received good news: Toyotaki might be reaching Baku Customs soon. So I quickly moved out, took a taxi, and relocated to Black Mount Hotel near the customs area.

这一天的节奏很特别：早上，我在老城区看一座城市的古老时间；下午，我又被现实拉回 customs、taxi、hotel、等待车辆抵达的压力。Baku 对我来说，就是这样打开的：一边是历史，一边是物流。

The rhythm of that day was unusual: in the morning, I saw the ancient time of a city in the old quarter; in the afternoon, reality pulled me back into customs, taxi rides, hotels, and the pressure of waiting for my vehicle. This was how Baku opened to me: history on one side, logistics on the other.
