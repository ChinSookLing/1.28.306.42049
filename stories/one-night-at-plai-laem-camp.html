One Night at Plai Laem Camp

Plai Laem Camp 的一夜

21 August，Thailand 的 road rhythm 继续往南。Toyotaki 停在 ปลายแหลมแคมป์ campsite，Songkhla, Thailand。夜色下来以后，营地的灯、白色栏杆、树和 gravel ground，把这一晚收成一个安静的 sleeping area。

On 21 August, Thailand’s road rhythm continued south. Toyotaki stopped at ปลายแหลมแคมป์ campsite in Songkhla, Thailand. After night fell, the campsite lights, white fence, trees, and gravel ground held this stop as a quiet sleeping area.

从 border 的紧绷，到火葬场旁边的雨夜，再到 Phitsanulok、Samut Sakhon、Phadang 的 camping rhythm，Thailand 这一段已经不再只是“进入一个国家”。它慢慢变成连续的 overnight map。

From the tension of the border, to the rainy night beside a crematorium, then into the camping rhythm of Phitsanulok, Samut Sakhon, and Phadang, this part of Thailand was no longer only about entering a country. It slowly became a continuous overnight map.

这一晚的记忆很简单：Toyotaki 停在灯光旁边，车窗里有自己的小生活，外面是夜色、树和营地的安静。很多 overland 的日子，就是这样一格一格地靠近回家的方向。

The memory of this night is simple: Toyotaki parked beside the lights, with its small life inside the windows, and outside, the night, trees, and the quiet of a campsite. Many overland days move toward home exactly like this — one small frame at a time.

所以这篇不是景点。它是 Thailand 南下路上的另一晚：不惊险，不喧闹，只是把车停好，然后继续等明天。

So this is not a sightseeing stop. It is another night on the southbound road through Thailand: not dramatic, not loud, just parking the van and waiting for tomorrow.
