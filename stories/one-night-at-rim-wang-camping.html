One Night at Rim Wang Camping

Rim Wang Camping 的一夜

19 August，Thailand 的 overnight rhythm 继续往南。Toyotaki 停在 Rim Wang Camping，Khok Kham, Mueang Samut Sakhon, Thailand。树、水边、草地和黄昏后的光，让这一晚成为很安静的一格。

On 19 August, Thailand’s overnight rhythm continued south. Toyotaki stopped at Rim Wang Camping in Khok Kham, Mueang Samut Sakhon, Thailand. Trees, water, grass, and the light after dusk made this night a quiet frame in the route.

Location
Rim Wang Camping / 7 72ม.9, Khok Kham, Mueang Samut Sakhon 74000 / Thailand
Route Node
Thailand / Rim Wang Camping Sleeping Area
Date
19 August 2024

前两天的 Thailand 是 border、规则、雨夜和火葬场旁边的小林子。到了这一晚，画面慢慢换成水边和树影。Toyotaki 停在草地上，旁边有建筑、有水、有风，也有夜色慢慢落下来。

The first two days of Thailand had been border rules, rain, and a little grove beside a crematorium. By this night, the scene shifted toward water and tree shadows. Toyotaki parked on grass, with buildings, water, wind, and the night slowly settling around it.

不是每一晚都需要一个 dramatic title。有些晚上只是把车停好，把身体放下，然后让路继续在明天发生。Rim Wang Camping 的这一夜，就是 Thailand 进入日常节奏后的一个安静节点。

Not every night needs a dramatic title. Some nights are simply about parking the van, putting the body down, and letting the road continue tomorrow. This night at Rim Wang Camping was one quiet node as Thailand entered its daily rhythm.
