Thailand · Huay Xai Border · 17 August 2024

Thailand Opened with Rain, Rules, and a Night Beside the Crematorium

泰国在雨、规则和火葬场旁的一夜中打开

17 August，Thailand 终于打开了。这是第 28 个国家。可是它不是轻轻松松打开的：雨、border rules、motorhome restriction、Malaysian overlanders，还有一个睡在火葬场旁边的夜晚，全都一起进来了。

On 17 August, Thailand finally opened. It was the twenty-eighth country. But it did not open easily: rain, border rules, motorhome restrictions, Malaysian overlanders, and a night sleeping beside a crematorium all entered the memory together.

Location
Thailand Border / Huay Xai / AH-3 and 1020 Highway
Route Node
Thailand Opening / Border Rules and Rainy Sleeping Area

终于来到第二十八个国家。路面还湿着，天空还是阴的，Toyotaki 站在 Thailand border 前面。一路从 Malaysia 出发，经过那么多国家，这一刻当然值得记下来。

Finally, the twenty-eighth country. The road was still wet, the sky still grey, and Toyotaki stood in front of the Thailand border. After leaving Malaysia and passing through so many countries, this moment deserved to be marked.

可是泰国把关很严。Motorhome 一律不准进。我只能一直向 customs 强调：我的不是 motorhome，是 van，是 VAN。最后 Toyotaki 才被允许进入。

But Thailand’s border control was strict. Motorhomes were not allowed in. I had to keep emphasizing to customs that mine was not a motorhome — it was a van, a VAN. Only then was Toyotaki allowed through.

就在会晒边境，我遇见了马来西亚的 Klustermotorhome。他们从 Dubai、Mongolia、China、Laos 回来，在这个边境停留了一个星期。当天，他们终于被允许使用拖车，从 Huay Xai 一路开往 Black Mountain。

At the Huay Xai border, I met the Malaysian Klustermotorhome group. They were returning from Dubai, Mongolia, China, and Laos, and had been stuck at this border for a full week. That day, they were finally allowed to use towing from Huay Xai to Black Mountain.

那一晚，我沿 AH-3 / 1020 highway 从 Huay Xai 往南开，最后停在路边的 bushes 里。雨下了一整夜，我睡不好。后来同学告诉我，17 August 2024 正好是农历七月十四，也就是民间说的鬼门开。

That night, I drove south from Huay Xai along the AH-3 / 1020 highway and stopped in the bushes beside the road. It rained all night, and I could not sleep. Later, my classmate told me that 17 August 2024 was the fourteenth day of the seventh lunar month, known in folk belief as the opening of the ghost gate.

这就是 Thailand opening：不是旅游广告里的阳光沙滩，而是 border rules、VAN 的解释、同乡 motorhome 的相遇、拖车许可、雨夜、火葬场旁边的 bushes，和一整晚睡不着。

This was the opening of Thailand: not the sunshine and beaches of a travel advertisement, but border rules, the explanation of “VAN,” a meeting with Malaysian motorhome travellers, towing permission, rain, bushes beside a crematorium, and a sleepless night.
