Russia · Saratov / Ozinki / Volgograd Road · 21–22 June 2024

The 300 km Back to My Documents

为了证件，开回 300 km

这篇 memory 不是关于风景，而是关于 passport、immigration documents、现金余额、300 km 回头路，以及一个愿意帮忙的 Nataliya。

This memory is not about scenery. It is about a passport, immigration documents, cash balance, a 300 km return drive, and Nataliya, who tried to help.

Location
Saratov MID / Saratov Immigration Dept / Mini Hotel U Pruda / Volgograd road
Route Node
Russia · Documents and Border Rules
Date
21–22 June 2024

6 月 21 日早上，我一醒来就开去 MID Saratov，也就是 Saratov Region 的 Ministry of Foreign Affairs。很幸运，有一位 lady officer 愿意接待我。她人很好，后来还用 WhatsApp 跟我联系。

On the morning of 21 June, I drove to MID Saratov, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs office for the Saratov Region. Luckily, one lady officer was willing to attend to me. She was very kind and later stayed in contact with me through WhatsApp.

可是她也不能帮太多，因为我必须按照 Russia customs 的规则走。她建议我去附近的 immigration department 再确认一次。我去了，也在那里犯下一个后来才发现的大错误。

But even she could not help much, because I had to follow Russian customs rules. She recommended that I check with the nearby immigration department. I went there, and that was where I made a big mistake that I would only discover later.

那天路上，我不小心远远拍到一个疑似敏感设施的地方，也看到直升机在降落。iPhone 拍出来远远不及眼睛看到的清楚。我很快意识到不应该停留，马上收起手机，lap 油走人。

That day, from far away, I accidentally photographed what looked like a sensitive facility, and I also saw a helicopter landing. The iPhone image was nowhere near as clear as what my eyes saw. I quickly realised that I should not stay there, put the phone away, and drove off immediately.

当晚我开了大约 300 km，在 Mini Hotel U Pruda 的 parking 过夜。那时候我还不知道，最重要的 passport 和 immigration documents 已经不在我身边。

That night, I drove about 300 km and stayed overnight at the parking area of Mini Hotel U Pruda. At that moment, I still did not know that my most important passport and immigration documents were no longer with me.

6 月 22 日早上醒来时，我先觉得前座有点不对劲。后来喝了一杯 coffee，大约早上 10:30，我才突然意识到：我的 passport 和 immigration documents 留在 Saratov immigration department 了。

On the morning of 22 June, when I woke up, I first felt that something was wrong with the front seat. Later, after having a coffee, at around 10:30 a.m., I suddenly realised that I had left my passport and immigration documents at the Saratov immigration department.

我马上联系 Nataliya，也就是 MID office 的那位 officer，告诉她这件事。当时我身上只有大约 8300 rub cash balance。我决定马上开回去，重新走那 300 km。

I immediately contacted Nataliya, the officer from the MID office, to tell her what had happened. At that time, I had about 8,300 rubles of cash left. I decided to drive back immediately, repeating the 300 km road.

下午 3 点，Nataliya 回 WhatsApp 给我。她成功联系到 security guard，他们找到了我的重要文件。那一刻，我才真的开始呼吸。

At 3 p.m., Nataliya replied on WhatsApp. She had managed to contact the security guard, and they had found my important documents. At that moment, I could finally breathe again.

我在下午 5:42pm 赶到 immigration department，拿回我的 passport 和 immigration documents。然后我又继续赶路，再开大约 380 km，到 Volgograd 的 Astoriya petrol station 过夜。

I reached the immigration department at 5:42 p.m. and collected my passport and immigration documents. Then I rushed back onto the road again, driving another 380 km to overnight at Astoriya petrol station in Volgograd.

What a day。那一天让我知道，在长途 solo overland 里，证件不是文件而已。证件是路还可以继续走的资格。

What a day. That day taught me that in a long solo overland journey, documents are not just papers. They are the right to continue moving.
