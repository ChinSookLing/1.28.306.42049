China · Toyotaki Repair Node · 6 August 2024

The Air Filter Tied with Wire

用铁丝绑回去的 air filter

6 August 的 sleeping area，是里拉春天酒店停车场，中国。可是这一天进入 archive 的，不只是 sleeping area，而是 Toyotaki 又一次用 overland 的方式活下来。

The sleeping area on 6 August was Lila Spring Hotel Parking, China. But what entered the archive that day was not only the sleeping area. It was another moment when Toyotaki survived in an overland way.

Location
Lila Spring Hotel Parking / China
Route Node
China / Toyotaki Survival Repair
Date
6 August 2024

前一晚倒车时，我不小心撞到矮短墙。不是很大的事故，可是对一辆走了这么远的 campervan 来说，小撞一下也会变成真实问题：air filter 掉下来了。

The previous night, while reversing, I accidentally hit a short low wall. It was not a big accident, but for a campervan that had travelled this far, even a small impact could become a real problem: the air filter dropped off.

中国没有我的 model。这个句子很短，但在 overland 里面很重：当车在异国出问题，不是每个零件都能马上找到，不是每个修法都漂亮。

China did not have my model. It is a short sentence, but in overland travel it carries weight: when a vehicle has a problem in another country, not every part can be found immediately, and not every repair can be elegant.

所以只能先请老板用铁丝绑一下。不是完美修理，不是原厂方案，但它让 Toyotaki 可以继续走。很多时候，overland 的逻辑就是这样：先固定住，先不要掉，先能走到下一站。

So the shop boss temporarily tied it with wire. It was not a perfect repair, not an original-part solution, but it allowed Toyotaki to keep going. Many times, overland logic is like this: secure it first, keep it from falling first, make it possible to reach the next stop first.

这一篇把 China 章从景点、酒店、签证和 city-centre heat 拉回 Toyotaki 本身。路不是只有风景，路也包括车身下面那些临时的、土法的、但很必要的 survival repair。

This memory pulls the China chapter back from sights, hotels, visas, and city-centre heat to Toyotaki itself. The road is not only scenery. It also includes those temporary, improvised, but necessary survival repairs under the vehicle.
