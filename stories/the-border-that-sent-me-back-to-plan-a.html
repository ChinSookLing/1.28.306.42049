Russia · APP Burgristoye / Ozinki · 19–20 June 2024

The Border That Sent Me Back to Plan A

不能从这里出境，只好回到 Plan A

19 June 是 sauna、shower 和 laundry；20 June 是 Kazakhstan border 告诉我 e-visa 不能从这里出境。于是 Plan A 回来了：先去 Georgia，多走 1500 km。

19 June was sauna, shower, and laundry. 20 June was the Kazakhstan border telling me that my e-visa could not exit there. So Plan A returned: Georgia first, with another 1,500 km added.

Location
APP Burgristoye → Ozinki parking / Russia
Route Node
Russia · E-visa Border Reversal
Date
19–20 June 2024

6 月 19 日，我去了 city pool。对一个长途 solo overlander 来说，sauna、shower 和 laundry 不是小事，那是身体重新整理自己的地方。

On 19 June, I went to a city pool. For a long-distance solo overlander, sauna, shower, and laundry are not small things. They are where the body reorganises itself.

那位 lady owner 允许我在那里过夜，因为第二天我要去 APP Burgristoye，试试看能不能用我的 e-visa 从那个边境过 Kazakhstan。

The lady owner allowed me to stay overnight there because the next day I needed to go to APP Burgristoye to try whether I could cross that border into Kazakhstan with my e-visa.

结果当然不可以。E-visa 对入境和出境口岸有严格监控，不是想从哪里出就可以从哪里出。20 June 到 Kazakhstan border 时，officer 告诉我不能用 e-visa 从那里出去。

Of course, it did not work. The e-visa had strict monitoring on entry and exit ports; I could not simply exit from any border I wanted. On 20 June, at the Kazakhstan border, the officer told me that I could not leave through that port with the e-visa.

所以我只能回到 Plan A：先去 Georgia。这个决定让我多走大约 1500 km，而当时我身上的 Russia cash 已经有限。

So I had to return to Plan A: go to Georgia first. That decision added about 1,500 km to the road, while my Russia cash was already limited.

那一晚的 sleeping area 是 Zemlyakam Ozintsam Uchastnikam Voyn I Lokal'nykh Konfliktov I Likvidatoram Stikhiynykh Bedstviy I Katastrof, Ozinki parking。名字很长，路也突然变长。

That night’s sleeping area was Zemlyakam Ozintsam Uchastnikam Voyn I Lokal'nykh Konfliktov I Likvidatoram Stikhiynykh Bedstviy I Katastrof, Ozinki parking. The name was long, and the road suddenly became long too.
