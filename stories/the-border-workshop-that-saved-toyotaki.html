The Border Workshop That Saved Toyotaki

在老挝边境救回 Toyotaki 的地下车库

12 August，Toyotaki 抵达老挝边境。但这不是一个轻松的 country opening。真正进入老挝的，是一辆 clutch 已经开始出问题、air filter 也还需要修的 campervan。

On 12 August, Toyotaki reached the Laos border. But this was not an easy country opening. What entered Laos was a campervan whose clutch had already started failing, with the air filter still needing repair too.

Location
Laos Border / Near Boten
Route Node
Laos / Border Workshop and Toyotaki Repair
Date
12–15 August 2024

其实从 11 August 在 Jinghong 附近开始，clutch 已经一阵一阵不对劲。有时我踩下去，它没有好好弹回来。那种感觉不是普通担心，是心慢慢沉下去：如果 clutch 真坏，Toyotaki 可能就停在路上了。

Since 11 August, near Jinghong, the clutch had already started behaving intermittently. Sometimes when I stepped on it, the pedal did not come back properly. That feeling was not ordinary worry. My heart was sinking: if the clutch truly failed, Toyotaki might stop on the road.

到了老挝边境，问题变得更严重。坏运气是：Toyotaki 真的需要修。好运气是：边境附近刚好有一位中国老板的 workshop。他可以从昆明订货，也愿意帮我处理 clutch 和 air filter。

By the time I reached the Laos border, the problem became more serious. The bad luck was that Toyotaki truly needed repair. The good luck was that near the border, there was a workshop run by a Chinese boss. He could order parts from Kunming and was willing to help with both the clutch and the air filter.

零件要从昆明来，所以不能马上走。12 August 到 15 August，我就住在 Toyotaki 里面，而 Toyotaki 停在他的地下车库 workshop 里。那不是营地，也不是酒店，但那几晚，它就是最安全的地方。

The parts had to come from Kunming, so I could not leave immediately. From 12 to 15 August, I stayed inside Toyotaki while it was parked in the underground workshop. It was not a campsite and not a hotel, but for those nights, it was the safest place.

这就是 overland 很真实的一面：有时候你不是在选择景点，而是在等零件。有时候不是幸运或倒霉二选一，而是两个同时发生。clutch 在边境坏掉，是倒霉；刚好遇到能帮忙的老板，是幸运。

This is one very real side of overland travel: sometimes you are not choosing sights, you are waiting for parts. Sometimes luck and bad luck do not come separately; they happen at the same time. Having the clutch fail at the border was bad luck. Meeting a boss who could help was luck.

这一篇也是 Laos 的 opening。老挝不是从风景开始，而是从一个地下车库开始：Toyotaki 被救回来，路才可以继续。

This memory is also the opening of Laos. Laos did not begin with scenery. It began inside an underground workshop: Toyotaki had to be saved before the road could continue.
