China · Tianshui to Visa-Run Pivot · 27–29 July 2024

The Camp Before the Visa Run

签证折返点前的露营夜

27 July 的 sleeping area 是漫时露营俱乐部，天水，中国。灯光很温柔，帐篷倒映在水面上，Toyotaki 停在黑夜里。看起来像一个安静的 camping night，但其实 visa clock 已经在靠近。

The sleeping area on 27 July was Manshi Camping Club in Tianshui, China. The lights were gentle, the tents reflected on the water, and Toyotaki rested in the dark. It looked like a quiet camping night, but the visa clock was already getting closer.

Location
Tianshui → Xi’an / China
Route Node
China / Tianshui Camp and Visa-Run Pivot
Date
27–29 July 2024

那一晚的漫时露营俱乐部很漂亮。小帐篷沿着水边亮起来，像路上的一个 soft pause。中国段前面有高温、酒店登记、沙漠 karaoke 和高海拔；到了这里，画面突然安静下来。

Manshi Camping Club was beautiful that night. Small tents lit up along the water, like a soft pause on the road. The China section had already carried heat, hotel registration, desert karaoke, and higher altitude; here, the scene suddenly became quiet.

Toyotaki 停在树林和灯光之间。它还是那个移动的家，但接下来两天，我要暂时离开 group，自己处理一件很现实的事：China visa-free 的时间。

Toyotaki parked between trees and lights. It was still my moving home, but in the next two days I would temporarily leave the group to handle something very practical: the China visa-free clock.

我是 Malaysian，当时用 30 days visa-free 进入中国。所以我必须离开中国到 Hong Kong，再 same day 回来。这个决定把 team phase 切开了一下：我人在队伍里，但规则要求我短暂地一个人行动。

As a Malaysian, I was using the 30-day visa-free entry to China at that time. So I had to leave China for Hong Kong and return on the same day. That requirement cut into the team phase: I was travelling with the group, but the rule required me to move alone for a short while.

29 July，我一个人离开 group，开去西安咸阳国际机场。那不是旅行中的景点，而是路线被规则折回去的一刻：从 team road 暂时转成 solo administrative road。

On 29 July, I left the group alone and drove to Xi’an Xianyang International Airport. It was not a sightseeing stop. It was the moment when the route folded back because of rules: from team road into a temporary solo administrative road.

所以这篇不是普通露营夜。它是 China team phase 里的一个转折点：我已经学着和别人一起走，但签证时间提醒我，有些路段还是必须自己处理。

So this was not just an ordinary camping night. It was a turning point inside the China team phase: I was learning to move with others, but the visa clock reminded me that some sections still had to be handled alone.
