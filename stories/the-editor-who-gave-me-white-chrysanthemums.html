The Editor Who Gave Me White Chrysanthemums

送我白色菊花的 Kazakhstan Editor

12 July，我终于见到我的 Kazakhstan editor 朋友。他平时帮我 edit YouTube channel 的 video，所以这次见面不是普通的路人相遇，而是一个线上帮忙很久的人，终于在 Kazakhstan 变成真实的人。

On 12 July, I finally met my Kazakhstan editor friend. He had been helping me edit videos for my YouTube channel, so this was not just a random road encounter. Someone who had helped me online finally became a real person in Kazakhstan.

Location
Karabulak / Kazakhstan
Route Node
Kazakhstan · Long-run Mode / Human Hospitality
Date
12 July 2024

根据他那边的习俗，他送了我一束白色菊花。对我来说，这束花很特别：在一段每天赶路、找停车点、看油量和公里数的 long-run mode 里面，突然有人用花来迎接我。

Following the custom on his side, he gave me a bouquet of white chrysanthemums. To me, the flowers felt special: inside a long-run mode of driving, parking, fuel, and kilometres, someone suddenly welcomed me with flowers.

那天晚上，我住在 Otabek’s house at Karabulak, Kazakhstan。路上的 hospitality 有时候不是大事件，而是一张桌子、一杯茶、一家人坐在一起，让长途驾驶的人暂时不用再一个人撑着。

That night, I stayed at Otabek’s house in Karabulak, Kazakhstan. Road hospitality is not always a big event. Sometimes it is a table, tea, and a family sitting together, allowing a long-distance driver to stop carrying everything alone for a while.

这篇 memory 是 Kazakhstan long-run mode 里面很柔软的一页。前面是 ferry、Kuryk、动物过马路、E38 蝗虫；这里突然变成了花、朋友、家、晚上的安顿。

This memory is a softer page inside Kazakhstan’s long-run mode. Before this came the ferry, Kuryk, animals crossing the road, and locusts on the E38. Here, the road suddenly became flowers, friendship, a home, and a place to settle for the night.
