Thailand · Wan Thong, Phitsanulok · 18 August 2024

The First Calm Night After Thailand Opened

泰国打开后的第一个安稳夜

前一晚，是 AH-3 / 1020 highway 旁边的小林子、雨、火葬场，以及一整夜睡不好。18 August，Toyotaki 终于停进一个真正的 camping ground：ชมจันทร์ แคมป์​ปิ้ง，Wan Thong, Phitsanulok, Thailand。

The night before had been a little grove beside the AH-3 / 1020 highway, rain, a crematorium, and a sleepless night. On 18 August, Toyotaki finally stopped at a real camping ground: ชมจันทร์ แคมป์​ปิ้ง, Wan Thong, Phitsanulok, Thailand.

Location
ชมจันทร์ แคมป์​ปิ้ง camping ground / Wan Thong, Phitsanulok / Thailand
Route Node

Date
18 August 2024

这不是 border，不是 workshop，也不是路边临时找地方躲雨。这里有草地、有树、有灯。Toyotaki 停在灯光和树影之间，Thailand 的第一段 tension 慢慢松下来。

This was not a border, not a workshop, and not a roadside place found in the rain. There was grass, there were trees, and there were lights. Toyotaki parked between the warm lights and the shadows of the trees, and the first layer of Thailand tension slowly softened.

有些 overnight memory 不是因为发生了大事才重要，而是因为它终于没有再发生什么。前一晚还在雨里的火葬场旁边，下一晚已经能停在有灯的草地上。路没有突然变容易，可是身体终于可以先放下来。

Some overnight memories matter not because something dramatic happened, but because finally, nothing else happened. One night had been beside a crematorium in the rain; the next night was on grass under lights. The road had not suddenly become easy, but the body could finally settle down for a while.

所以这篇不是一个景点。它是 Thailand 打开之后，第一个比较安稳的夜晚。

So this is not a sightseeing stop. It is the first calmer night after Thailand opened.
