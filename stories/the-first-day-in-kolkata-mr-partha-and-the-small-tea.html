The First Day in Kolkata: Mr Partha and the Small Tea

Kolkata 的第一天：Mr Partha 和那杯小茶

Kolkata 的第一天，我还没有 Toyotaki。Toyotaki 几乎已经抵达 Kolkata，但还在 shipping / port process 里。我在等 shipping company，等 Toyotaki 到港后，他们要来向我收车钥匙和文件。

On my first day in Kolkata, I did not have Toyotaki yet. Toyotaki had almost reached Kolkata, but it was still inside the shipping and port process. I was waiting for the shipping company to collect the car key and documents once the vehicle arrived.

India First Day / Waiting for Toyotaki

我到了 India 后，好像变成另一个人。我很想和 local 说话，想知道他们怎样生活。我的 India 第一日，不是从 highway 开始，而是从走路、声音、街道和人的生活开始。

After arriving in India, I felt like I became a different person. I wanted to talk to local people and understand more about their lives. My first day in India did not begin on the highway; it began on foot, with sounds, streets, and everyday life.

就在一个 community toilet 前，我遇见了 Mr Partha。他二十多岁，是 electrical engineering student。他的工作，是照顾这个厕所。牌子上写着英文：PAY & USE TOILET。厕所早上 6 点开，收费 Rs2 和 Rs5。

In front of a community toilet, I met Mr Partha. He was in his twenties, an electrical engineering student. His job was to take care of the toilet. The sign was in English: PAY & USE TOILET. The toilet opened at 6am, and people paid Rs2 or Rs5.

Toyotaki 还没回到我手上，所以这一天不是 driving day。可是现在回头看，它仍然是 road story 的一部分：因为在方向盘之前，India 先用人、声音、茶和一块 PAY & USE TOILET 的牌子，向我打开了第一扇门。

Toyotaki was not back in my hands yet, so this was not a driving day. But looking back, it was still part of the road story: before the steering wheel, India first opened through people, sound, tea, and a PAY & USE TOILET sign.
