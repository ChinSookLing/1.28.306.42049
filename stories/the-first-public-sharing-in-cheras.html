The First Public Sharing in Cheras

Cheras 的第一次公开分享

20 October 2024，路已经回来了，但故事还没有结束。第一次公开分享在 Cheras 的 Oh Yeah Kopitiam 三楼举行：1.2.3.4 Asia to Iceland Campervan Trip。

On 20 October 2024, the road had returned, but the story had not ended. The first public sharing took place on the third floor of Oh Yeah Kopitiam in Cheras: 1.2.3.4 Asia to Iceland Campervan Trip.

Location
Oh Yeah Kopitiam / 100G Jalan Cerdas, Taman Connaught, 56000 Kuala Lumpur
Route Node
After the Return / First Public Sharing
Date
20 October 2024

这一次，Toyotaki 不在路上，而在投影里。地图、照片、故事、危险、修车、边境、等待和回家，都被放到一间房间里，让别人一起听。

This time, Toyotaki was not on the road, but on the screen. Maps, photos, stories, danger, repairs, borders, waiting, and the return home were brought into one room for others to hear.

这篇不是新的旅程节点，而是旅程的回声：路走完以后，它开始被讲述、被提问，也被别人带回自己的生活里。

This is not a new travel node, but an echo of the journey: after the road ended, it began to be spoken, questioned, and carried into other people’s lives.
