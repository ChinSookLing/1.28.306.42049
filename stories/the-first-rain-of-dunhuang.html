China · Dunhuang · 22–23 July 2024

The First Rain of Dunhuang

敦煌的第一场雨

前一天，我才开玩笑地夸口：我来自大马，是稀客，所以我来了就会下雨。结果第二天早上，敦煌真的下雨了。

The day before, I had jokingly boasted: I came from Malaysia, a rare guest here, so when I arrived, it would rain. The next morning, Dunhuang really had rain.

Location
Dunhuang / China
Route Node
China / Dunhuang Rain and Foreigner Hotel Registration
Date
22–23 July 2024

对我这个马来西亚人来说，那当然不算什么大雨。可是对敦煌人来说，那是“很大的雨”。更好笑的是，今早这场雨，正好是 2024 年敦煌的第一场雨。

For someone from Malaysia, of course it did not feel like heavy rain. But in the eyes of people in Dunhuang, it was “big rain.” The funniest part was that this morning’s rain was also the first rain of Dunhuang in 2024, according to what I was told there.

22 July 和 23 July 的 sleeping area，是 TOWO 上品酒店（敦煌市政府沙州夜市店），敦煌，中国。进入新疆和这段中国西北路线后，住宿不再只是“哪里方便停车”的问题。

The sleeping area on 22 and 23 July was TOWO Shangpin Hotel, Dunhuang Municipal Government Shazhou Night Market branch, Dunhuang, China. After entering Xinjiang and this northwestern China section, accommodation was no longer only a question of where parking was convenient.

按照我们当时被告知和实际经历的当地安保登记要求，foreign tourists 必须住在有资格接待外国游客的酒店。酒店需要把我们的资料详细登记，并向当地派出所申报。

As we were told and as we experienced under local security registration requirements, foreign tourists had to stay in hotels qualified to receive foreign guests. The hotel had to register our details carefully and report them to the local police station.

所以这一段中国路，不只是火焰山、高温、食物和团队。它也有很现实的程序：哪里可以住，谁可以接待，资料怎样登记。对 overlander 来说，这些都是路线的一部分。

So this China section was not only about the Flaming Mountains, heat, food, and team travel. It also had very practical procedures: where we could stay, which hotels could receive us, and how our details had to be registered. For overlanders, these procedures were also part of the route.

但在这些现实程序之间，敦煌还是留下了一个很轻的小笑话：我说我来自大马，所以会下雨；第二天，沙漠城市真的给了我一场雨。

But between all these practical procedures, Dunhuang still left me with a small joke: I said I came from Malaysia, so it would rain. The next day, the desert city really gave me rain.
