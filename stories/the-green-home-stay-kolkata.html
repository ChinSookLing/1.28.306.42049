A Different Kind of Waiting

When the campervan was still behind the Customs gate.

Toyotaki had not been released yet. So the journey continued without the campervan.

Tuzi moved from the previous homestay to Green Home Stay. What could have been just another temporary place to sleep became another small window into Kolkata.

The owner did not simply provide a room. He brought Tuzi outside, walked around Kolkata, introduced local food, and shared his own love for India.

Sometimes a journey moves because the vehicle moves. Sometimes it moves because a person opens a door.

Hot Biryani · Cold Yogurt

One of the strangest food combinations I remember.

The previous homestay gave Tuzi biryani rice together with yogurt. The rice was hot. The yogurt was cold.

It was such a simple combination, but it stayed in the memory. One day, Tuzi hopes to try this combination again in Malaysia.

Maybe that is how travel memories survive: not through grand monuments, but through a plate of hot rice and something cold beside it.
