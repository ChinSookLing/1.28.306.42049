The Heat of City-Centre Nights

市中心夜晚的热

1 August 的 sleeping area，是阆中市三联书院客栈，阆中，中国。这个地方本身很漂亮，可是这一天真正进入 archive 的，不只是客栈，而是我开始很清楚地感觉到：group travel 的逻辑和 solo overland 的逻辑不一样。

The sleeping area on 1 August was Sanlian Shuyuan Guesthouse in Langzhong, China. The place itself was beautiful, but what entered the archive that day was not only the guesthouse. It was the clear feeling that group travel and solo overland travel follow different logic.

Location
Langzhong / Nanchong / China
Route Node
China / City-Centre Heat and Group Travel Tension
Date
1 August 2024

我这个团，除了我以外，大家都要求去看景点，也要求住在市区中心。市区当然方便，可是在四川的热里，树都没几棵，人像被烤焦一样。

In this group, everyone except me wanted to visit sightseeing spots and stay in city centres. City centres were convenient, of course, but in the Sichuan heat there were so few trees, and people felt as if they were being roasted.

这和我之前 solo journey 的习惯完全不同。一个人开 Toyotaki 的时候，我很少住市区。我比较习惯睡在 campervan 里：park、roadside、campsite、树下、河边，哪里有风，哪里有一点自然遮荫，哪里就像可以喘气。

This was completely different from my solo journey habits. When I travelled alone with Toyotaki, I rarely stayed in town. I was used to sleeping in the campervan: in parks, by roadsides, at campsites, under trees, beside rivers. Wherever there was wind, shade, or a little natural space, I felt I could breathe.

可是进入 group 以后，路线开始被景点和市区酒店牵着走。我一连住了几天酒店。身体不是不懂感恩，而是它记得以前那种自由停车的节奏。

But after entering the group phase, the route began to be pulled by sightseeing spots and city hotels. I stayed in hotels for several consecutive days. My body was not ungrateful; it simply remembered the rhythm of free parking from before.

所以这篇不是在抱怨酒店，也不是在说城市不好。它是在记录一种 travel logic changed 的感觉：从一个人找树、找水、找停车的路，变成跟着团队、景点、市中心和酒店走。

So this memory is not a complaint about hotels, and not a statement that cities are bad. It records the feeling that the travel logic had changed: from a solo road of finding trees, water, and parking, into a group road shaped by sightseeing spots, city centres, and hotels.

China team phase 让我学习和别人一起走。可是这一晚也提醒我：学习一起走，不代表身体会立刻忘记自己曾经怎样一个人活在路上。

The China team phase taught me to move with others. But this night also reminded me: learning to move together does not mean the body immediately forgets how it used to live alone on the road.
