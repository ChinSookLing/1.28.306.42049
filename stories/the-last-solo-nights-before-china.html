Kazakhstan · Final Solo Push · 13–16 July 2024

The Last Solo Nights Before China

进入中国前，最后的单打独斗夜晚

Kazakhstan 的最后几天，不再只是“赶到下一个停车点”。它变成一个阶段的结束：266 个单打独斗的日子，快要收起来了。

The final days in Kazakhstan were no longer only about reaching the next parking place. They became the closing of a phase: 266 days of travelling solo were about to be folded away.

Location
Kenen → Zharkent → near Horgos / Kazakhstan
Route Node
Kazakhstan · Final Solo Nights / Before China Team
Date
13–16 July 2024

13 July 清晨 5 点，我还在 Karabulak。长途路上的早晨有一种很特别的安静：天还没完全亮，身体已经知道今天又要继续往前。

At 5 a.m. on 13 July, I was still in Karabulak. Mornings on a long-distance road have a particular quietness: the day has not fully opened, but the body already knows it has to move again.

13 July 晚上，我停在 A2 Royal Petrol Station Parking, Kenen, Kazakhstan。对那时的我来说，petrol station parking 不只是停车场，而是当天可以结束的证据。

On the night of 13 July, I stopped at A2 Royal Petrol Station Parking, Kenen, Kazakhstan. At that point, a petrol station parking area was not just a parking place; it was proof that the day could end safely.

14 July，我继续推到 Zharkent，停在 Gastinitsa Lli Hotel。离 Horgos 越来越近，压力也越来越具体：不是“某一天会到”，而是日期已经贴到眼前。

On 14 July, I pushed on to Zharkent and stopped at Gastinitsa Lli Hotel. The closer I came to Horgos, the more concrete the pressure became: it was no longer “one day I will arrive,” but a date standing right in front of me.

15 July 和 16 July，我在 Гостиница Жеті Қазына parking。China team 的成员住在 hotel 里面，而我还是睡在 Toyotaki 的 parking。那一刻很微妙：我已经靠近团队，但身体还在自己的旧节奏里。

On 15 and 16 July, I stayed at Гостиница Жеті Қазына parking. The China team members stayed inside the hotel, while I still slept in Toyotaki in the parking area. It was a subtle moment: I was already near a team, but my body was still inside my old solo rhythm.

然后，是下一段的开始。266 个单打独斗的日子结束了。接下来的 26 天，我要学习和不同文化背景的人，一起跨越中国 5000 km。

Then, the next phase began. The 266 days of travelling solo had ended. For the next 26 days, I had to learn how to cross 5,000 km of China with people from different cultural backgrounds.

所以这篇不是 Kazakhstan 的风景篇，而是一个转场：从一个人扛着整条路，到终于要进入团队；从 Toyotaki parking 的孤独，到中国 5000 km 的共同旅程。

So this is not a scenery memory of Kazakhstan. It is a transition: from carrying the whole road alone to entering a team; from the solitude of Toyotaki’s parking nights to a shared 5,000 km journey across China.
