Russia · Kazanskiy Tambovskiy Muzhskoy Monastyr' · 18 June 2024

The Monastery Parking After Moscow

离开 Moscow 后，停在修道院旁

离开 Kremlin 附近的导航混乱之后，我往南移动，在一座漂亮的修道院旁边过夜。

After the navigation confusion near the Kremlin, I moved south and stayed overnight beside a beautiful monastery.

Location
Kazanskiy Tambovskiy Muzhskoy Monastyr' parking / Russia
Route Node
Russia · After Moscow Navigation
Date
18 June 2024

因为靠近 Kremlin 时 Google Map 不能可靠使用，我决定不要继续在那个范围里绕来绕去。我往南移动，避开让我不安心的区域，也避免一直拿着 Google Map 在敏感范围附近找路。

Because Google Maps could not be used reliably near the Kremlin area, I decided not to keep circling there. I moved south, away from the area that made me uneasy, and away from the need to keep checking Google Maps near a sensitive zone.

6 月 18 日，我在 Kazanskiy Tambovskiy Muzhskoy Monastyr' parking 过夜。这个地方很漂亮，教堂的颜色和傍晚的天空让 Toyotaki 的白色车身显得很安静。

On 18 June, I stayed overnight at Kazanskiy Tambovskiy Muzhskoy Monastyr' parking. It was a beautiful place. The colours of the church and the evening sky made Toyotaki’s white body feel quiet.

那一晚不是景点式停留，而是一种调整方向后的停靠。经历了 Moscow 的导航混乱之后，我需要一个能让自己重新安定下来的地方。

That night was not a tourist stop. It was a stop after changing direction. After the navigation confusion in Moscow, I needed a place where I could settle myself again.
