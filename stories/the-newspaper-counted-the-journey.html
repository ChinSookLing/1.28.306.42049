The Newspaper Counted the Journey

报纸如何计算这趟旅程

路回到 Malaysia 以后，它开始被别人提问、记录、剪裁和计算。光明日报采访时，用的是 27 countries 的算法：不包括 Malaysia，也把 Hong Kong 归入 China。

After the road returned to Malaysia, it began to be questioned, recorded, edited, and counted by others. In the Guang Ming Daily interview, the journey was counted as 27 countries: Malaysia was excluded, and Hong Kong was treated as part of China.

这不是错。它只是另一种 counting rule。媒体要用国家主权和版面逻辑来计算；archive 要保留一条路实际怎样被走过。

This was not wrong. It was simply another counting rule. The media counted through country status and editorial logic; the archive preserves how the road was actually travelled.

所以，在 1.28.306.42049 里，我保留自己的主轴：1 woman，28 countries，306 days，42,049 km。Hong Kong 是 China 下面的重要 visa / route node；Malaysia 是 final return country，因为回到 Bukit Kayu Hitam 后，我还带着妈妈继续走 Kedah → Ipoh → Melaka → home。

So in 1.28.306.42049, I keep the archive’s core identity: 1 woman, 28 countries, 306 days, 42,049 km. Hong Kong remains an important visa and route node under China; Malaysia is the final return country, because after Bukit Kayu Hitam I continued through Kedah, Ipoh, Melaka, and home with my mother.
