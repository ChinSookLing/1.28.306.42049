Georgia · Camp A Way · 26 June 2024

The Night Georgia Won

Georgia 赢了那一夜

我原本躺在床上，全身盖起来防蚊子。外面从晚上十点多一路吵到凌晨四点多：欢呼声、爆竹声、车笛声，还有我听起来像枪声的声音。那一刻我以为，这个镇是不是发生什么坏事了。

I was lying on the bed, covering my whole body because of mosquitoes. Outside, the noise continued from after 10 p.m. until after 4 a.m.: cheering, firecrackers, car horns, and sounds that I heard as gunshots. At that moment, I thought something bad might be happening in the town.

第二天早上，我跟 campsite owner 聊起来，才知道昨晚不是坏事，而是 Georgia 在 UEFA Euro 2024 以 2:0 赢了 Portugal。

The next morning, when I talked with the campsite owner, I finally learned that the night was not about something bad. Georgia had beaten Portugal 2–0 in UEFA Euro 2024.

这也让我重新理解那一晚的声音。对我这个外来者来说，那些声音像危险；对当地人来说，那是终于可以大声庆祝的时刻。

It made me understand the night differently. To me, as an outsider, those sounds first felt dangerous. To the locals, they were the sound of a long-awaited celebration.

在等待期间，我也帮 campsite 主人去打扫和整理她姐姐的旧屋，因为那间屋子准备出售。长途旅行有时不是一直移动，而是在一个地方停下来，变成临时生活的一部分。

During the waiting period, I also helped the campsite owner clean and arrange her sister’s old house, because the house was going to be sold. Long travel is not always movement. Sometimes it means stopping in one place and becoming part of a temporary life.

所以这篇不是足球新闻，而是一段很奇怪也很真实的 road memory：一个晚上我以为镇上出事，第二天才知道自己见证了 Georgia 的欢庆；同一段等待里，我也帮别人整理旧屋，继续等我的 Plan C。

So this memory is not a football report. It is a strange and real road memory: one night I thought something had happened in town, and the next morning I learned that I had witnessed Georgia celebrating. In the same waiting period, I helped clean an old house and continued waiting for Plan C.
