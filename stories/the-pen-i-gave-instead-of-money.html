The Pen I Gave Instead of Money

我给出的不是钱，是一支笔

这篇 memory 没有现场照片，因为当时的环境敏感，不能随便拍照。它来自 dashcam、WhatsApp 记录和那一天身体记得的紧张。

This memory has no on-site photo because the situation was sensitive and I could not simply take pictures. It comes from dashcam memory, WhatsApp records, and the tension my body still remembers from that day.

Location
Vladikavkaz / North Ossetia–Alania / Road to Georgia / Mini Otel "Magistral" Parking
Route Node
Russia · Road to Georgia / Checkpoint Memory
Date
23 June 2024

6 月 23 日，我开了 797 km 往 border 方向去。目的地是 Vladikavkaz，North Ossetia–Alania Republic, Russia。那一天我真的很累。

On 23 June, I drove 797 km toward the border. The destination was Vladikavkaz, North Ossetia–Alania Republic, Russia. I was truly tired that day.

经过某个 gate 的时候，其中一位 officer 要求我付 2000 rub。我当时根本付不了，因为身上只剩大约 1000 rub cash。

While passing one gate, one of the officers asked me for 2,000 rubles. I could not pay it anyway, because I only had about 1,000 rubles of cash left.

我马上 WhatsApp call Nataliya。她帮我理解那边的情况。她转达给我的信息是：他们说我进入了一个 special territory，而这个区域需要 pass 才能进入，所以他们会为 administrative offense 起草 protocol。

I immediately called Nataliya through WhatsApp. She helped me understand what was happening. The message she received was that I had entered a special territory where entry required a pass, and because of that they would draw up a protocol for an administrative offense.

幸运的是，有一辆从 North Kazakhstan 来的车帮我翻译。那一刻，我不是靠一个人撑过去，而是靠 Nataliya 的远程帮助，加上路上陌生人的现场翻译。

Luckily, a vehicle from North Kazakhstan helped me translate. In that moment, I did not get through it alone. I had Nataliya helping remotely, and strangers on the road helping on-site.

经过双方帮助之后，他们最后让我离开。我以为这段已经结束了。

After help from both sides, they finally let me go. I thought that part was over.

结果开了大约 5 km，又有另一组 polis 拦下我。他们提出另一个不清楚的要求，金额从 100s 变成 50s。

But after driving about 5 km, another team of police stopped me. They made another unclear request, with the amount moving from the 100s to the 50s.

我真的没有钱可以给，也不想用钱解决这种不清楚的要求。所以我守住自己的做法：我没有给钱。我给他们一支印着 Tuzi Vlogs logo 的笔。

I really did not have money to give, and I did not want to solve an unclear request with money. So I held my practice: I did not give money. I gave them a pen with the Tuzi Vlogs logo instead.

那一支笔很小。可是对我来说，它代表一条线：我可以礼貌，我可以配合规则，我可以解释，但我不想用钱去换一个 shortcut。

The pen was small. But to me, it represented a line: I could be polite, I could follow rules, I could explain, but I did not want to exchange money for a shortcut.

最后他们也让我离开。我继续往前开，在 Mini Otel "Magistral" Parking 过夜。第二天还要去 Georgia，距离大约还有 134 km。

In the end, they also let me go. I continued driving and stayed overnight at Mini Otel "Magistral" Parking. The next day I still needed to go to Georgia, about another 134 km away.

我很期待 Georgia，因为那里终于可以 swipe credit card。那一天的我，已经不是想去哪里玩，而是很实际地想着：我需要过境，我需要油，我需要钱的入口，我需要继续走。

I was looking forward to Georgia because I could finally swipe my credit card there. By then, I was no longer thinking about where to visit. I was thinking practically: I needed to cross the border, I needed fuel, I needed access to money, and I needed to keep moving.
