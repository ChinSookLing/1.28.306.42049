The Real Welcome Home at Bukit Kayu Hitam

Bukit Kayu Hitam 真正的欢迎回家

Hat Yai 的 welcome banner，是 home before home。可是 25 August 到 Bukit Kayu Hitam Immigration 这一刻，才是真正的 welcome home。Toyotaki 回到 Malaysia，路也终于跨回自己的土地。

The welcome banner in Hat Yai was home before home. But on 25 August, at Bukit Kayu Hitam Immigration, the welcome home became real. Toyotaki returned to Malaysia, and the road finally crossed back onto home ground.

Location
Bukit Kayu Hitam Immigration / Kedah / Malaysia
Route Node
Malaysia / Final Return and Real Welcome Home
Date

这一趟旅程在网站里，我保留它最核心的身份：1 woman，28 countries，306 days，42,049 km。

For this archive, I keep the journey’s core identity: 1 woman, 28 countries, 306 days, 42,049 km.

这个数字背后有两个时间。Toyotaki 的 logistics timeline 比人先开始：10/10/2023 送到 Ample Warehouse，11/10/2023 被推入 container。Tuzi 真正一个人开始这段 overland，是 25/10/2023 从 KLIA 飞往 Kolkata；到 25/08/2024 回到 Malaysia，是 306 天。所以这趟路有车的出货时间，也有人的 306 天。

Behind the number are two timelines. Toyotaki’s logistics timeline began earlier: sent to Ample Warehouse on 10 October 2023, then pushed into the container on 11 October 2023. Tuzi’s solo overland journey began on 25 October 2023 with the flight from KLIA to Kolkata; when she returned to Malaysia on 25 August 2024, the human journey counted 306 days. This road carried both vehicle logistics time and human overland time.

数字也可以有不同算法。报社后来用 27 countries：不包括 Malaysia，也把 Hong Kong 归入 China。但在 1.28.306.42049 这个 archive 里，我保留 28 countries。Hong Kong 是 China 下面的重要 visa / route node；Malaysia 则是 final return country。

The numbers can be counted in different ways. A newspaper later used 27 countries, excluding Malaysia and treating Hong Kong as part of China. But in the 1.28.306.42049 archive, I keep 28 countries. Hong Kong remains an important visa and route node under China; Malaysia is the final return country.

因为 Malaysia 不是一过关就结束。回到 Bukit Kayu Hitam 后，我还带着妈妈继续走 Kedah → Ipoh → Melaka → home。旅程不是在 immigration counter 结束，而是在 Malaysia 这段 final return 之后，才真正回到家。

Because Malaysia was not just a passport endpoint. After crossing back at Bukit Kayu Hitam, I continued travelling with Mum through Kedah, Ipoh, Melaka, and then home. The journey did not end at the immigration counter; it ended only after the final return through Malaysia itself.

这篇就是终点的门。不是因为路从此没有以后，而是因为 306 天的那条线，终于在 Malaysia 被接住了。

This is the doorway of the ending. Not because the road had no future after this, but because the 306-day line was finally received by Malaysia.
