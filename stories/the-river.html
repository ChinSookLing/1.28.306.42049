India · Kolkata · Story 13

The River

Sometimes a place does not need much explanation. You just see it.

India Kolkata River Observation Reality

I was walking through Kolkata and came across the river.

I don't really have much to say about it.

I just saw it.

It made me think that there is still a lot of work to be done by the local municipal authorities.

Cities are not only their famous buildings, food and tourist spots. Sometimes the less comfortable things are part of what a traveller sees too.

I wasn't there to judge the city. I was simply walking through it.

And this was one of the things I saw.
