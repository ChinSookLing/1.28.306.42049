The SIM Card I Needed for Amazon

Sometimes a very ordinary online purchase sends you straight into everyday local life.

India Kolkata SIM Card Local Life

I wanted to purchase something from Amazon while I was in India.

There was just one small problem: I needed an Indian SIM card first.

So a very ordinary online purchase turned into a small trip into everyday Kolkata life.

I went into the Vi service centre and met the local staff. What could have been just another administrative errand became another little human moment on the journey.

Looking back, I like these small memories. They are not famous places, and there is nothing spectacular about getting a SIM card.

But travelling is also made of these little things: finding what you need, asking for help, meeting people, and somehow turning an ordinary errand into a memory.
