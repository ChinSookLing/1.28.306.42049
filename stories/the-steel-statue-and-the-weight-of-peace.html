The Steel Statue and the Weight of Peace

钢铁雕像前，和平的重量

这一天不是关于过关，也不是关于物资，而是关于一座巨大的 steel statue，以及一个来自和平国家的人站在它前面时感到的重量。

This day was not about the border or supplies. It was about a giant steel statue, and the weight felt by someone from a country where peace is deeply precious.

Location
Second World War Memorial → Camping Parus, Stara
Route Node
Russia · Peace and War Memory
Date
16 June 2024

6 月 16 日，我看见一座巨大的第二次世界大战纪念碑。最先震住我的，是它的钢铁感。那不是普通雕像的漂亮，而是一种从痛苦里锻出来的力量。

On 16 June, I saw a huge Second World War memorial. The first thing that stunned me was the feeling of steel. It was not the beauty of an ordinary statue, but a strength forged out of pain.

我来自一个把和平当成最重要资产的国家，所以当我第一次站在那座巨大的 steel statue 前面时，我被它里面的 aura 震住了。过了这么多年，那种重量仍然还在。

I come from a country where peace is one of the most important assets we have. So when I first stood before that giant steel statue, I was stunned by the aura within it. Even after all these years, the weight was still there.

那天晚上，我在 Russia 的 Camping Parus, Stara 过夜。天色开始暗下来，Toyotaki 停在草地和树林旁边。那一夜比较安静，像是 Russia 第一段路在慢慢收尾。

That night, I stayed overnight at Camping Parus, Stara, Russia. As the sky turned dark, Toyotaki rested beside the grass and trees. That night was quieter, as if the first Russia section of the road was slowly closing for the day.
