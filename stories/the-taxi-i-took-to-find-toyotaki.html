Russia · Moscow / Kremlin Area · 17 June 2024

The Taxi I Took to Find Toyotaki

我第一次搭的士去找 Toyotaki

这一天不是因为景点而留下来，而是因为 Google Map 在 Kremlin 附近严重 lag。最后，我第一次在旅途中搭的士，不是去一个地方，而是去找回自己的 Toyotaki。

This day stayed in memory not because of a landmark, but because Google Maps lagged badly near the Kremlin area. In the end, I took a taxi for the first time during the journey — not to visit a place, but to find Toyotaki again.

Location
Moscow / Kremlin area / roadside overnight
Route Node
Russia · Navigation System Failure
Date
17 June 2024

我到了 Kremlin / Red Square 一带。可是靠近 Kremlin 大概几公里范围时，我的 Google Map 开始严重 lag。当地人告诉我应该用 Yandex app，但那时候已经太迟了。

I reached the Kremlin / Red Square area. But within a few kilometres of the Kremlin, my Google Maps began to lag badly. A local person told me that I should use Yandex instead, but by then it was already too late.

我被迫把 Toyotaki 停得很远。我有记录停车位置，然后走过去 Kremlin。可是回程时，Google Map 还是 lag，有时候因为时间延迟，给我错的方向。

I was forced to park Toyotaki far away. I recorded the parking location and walked toward the Kremlin. But on the way back, Google Maps was still lagging, and sometimes the time delay gave me the wrong direction.

那一天我走了 23,584 步，还是走不到我泊车的地方。天越来越晚，我越来越确定：不是我不努力，是系统已经不能可靠地带我回去。

I walked 23,584 steps that day and still could not reach the place where I had parked. As the night came closer, I became more certain: it was not that I was not trying hard enough. The system could no longer reliably bring me back.

晚上 9:35pm，我终于求助路人，请她们用她们的 app 帮我打车。那是我第一次在旅行途中搭的士去找 Toyotaki。

At 9:35 p.m., I finally asked people nearby for help and asked them to use their app to call a taxi. It was the first time during the journey that I took a taxi to find Toyotaki.

这也是一种 road system memory。不是边境，不是油价，也不是 camping，而是一个导航系统失灵后，一个 solo traveller 要怎样把自己和自己的车重新接回一起。

This was also a road-system memory. Not a border, not fuel prices, not camping, but what a solo traveller had to do when a navigation system failed and she had to reconnect herself with her own vehicle.

17 June 那一晚，我就在路边过夜。不是计划好的浪漫停靠点，只是那一天终于找回 Toyotaki 以后，路给我的一个现实答案。

That night, 17 June, I stayed overnight by the roadside. It was not a planned romantic stop. It was simply the realistic answer the road gave me after I finally found Toyotaki again.
