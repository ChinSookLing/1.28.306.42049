The Terracotta Army and My AI Warrior Face

兵马俑和我的 AI 跪射俑脸

看了数十年的兵马俑，今天真的能亲眼目睹。以前它们在课本、电视、照片和想象里；这一天，它们终于在我眼前铺开。

After seeing the Terracotta Army in books, television, photos, and imagination for decades, I finally saw it with my own eyes. On this day, it spread out in front of me as a real place.

Location
Xi’an → Hanzhong / China
Route Node
China / Terracotta Army, AI Souvenir, and Hanzhong
Date
31 July 2024

下面的兵马俑很多，口字型的看台也同样人山人海。这个画面很好地证明了：兵马俑不是一个安静的历史空间，而是一个所有人都想亲眼确认的地方。

There were many terracotta warriors below, and the rectangular viewing galleries above were also packed with people. The scene proved something clearly: the Terracotta Army is not a quiet historical space; it is a place everyone wants to confirm with their own eyes.

然后，一个很现代的反差出现了：在西安秦王兵马俑，现在有 AI service，可以把游客的脸和兵马俑结合。于是，我也有了一个“AI 跪射俑脸”。

Then a very modern contrast appeared: at the Terracotta Warriors site in Xi’an, there was an AI service that could combine a visitor’s face with a terracotta warrior. So I also received my own “AI warrior face.”

这个 moment 很像中国这一章本身：古老和现代不断撞在一起。前面是杜甫草堂、兵马俑；旁边是 AI、航班、签证折返和高速公路。历史不是停在博物馆里，它和现代系统挤在同一条路上。

This moment felt like the China chapter itself: ancient and modern things kept colliding. Du Fu Thatched Cottage and the Terracotta Army stood beside AI, flights, visa runs, and highways. History was not staying inside museums; it was sharing the same road with modern systems.

31 July 的 sleeping area，是汉中红叶大酒店，汉中，中国。完成 Hong Kong same-day exit and return 后，路继续往前，Toyotaki 又回到地面上的中国路线。

The sleeping area on 31 July was Hanzhong Hongye Hotel in Hanzhong, China. After completing the Hong Kong same-day exit and return, the road continued, and Toyotaki returned to the China route on the ground.

所以这篇不只是兵马俑。它是一个很奇妙的 China road node：几十年的历史想象、现场的人山人海、AI 纪念服务，还有当晚停在汉中的 Toyotaki。

So this memory is not only about the Terracotta Army. It is a strange and vivid China road node: decades of historical imagination, crowds at the site, an AI souvenir service, and Toyotaki parked that night in Hanzhong.
