Estonia / Russia Border · Luhamaa / Sumilkino · 8–14 June 2024

The Ticket I Did Not Know I Needed

我不知道自己需要的一张票

这不是一篇景点故事，而是一篇边境系统故事：GoSwift、排队票、三天等待、冷冻货柜的噪音、俄罗斯海关检查，以及最后终于驶出 Sumilkino 边境时的松一口气。

This is not a sightseeing story. It is a border-system story: GoSwift, a queue ticket, three days of waiting, the noise of a cold-storage container, Russian customs inspection, and the relief of finally driving out through Sumilkino.

Location
Luhamaa Border Parking → Vastseliina Metskond Camping → Russian APP Sumilkino Border
Route Node
Latvia / Estonia · Baltic Gate
Date
8–14 June 2024

2024 年 6 月 8 日，我在 Luhamaa Border Parking 的 sleeping area。

On 8 June 2024, I was at the sleeping area of Luhamaa Border Parking.

那不是一个安静的夜晚。附近有一个冷冻货柜 24 小时开着，金属的 clinging 声一直在耳边、在头顶上方。我当时没有一直抱怨，可是那个声音是真的存在，压着夜晚，也压着 Toyotaki 里面想休息的我。

It was not a quiet night. A cold-storage container nearby was running twenty-four hours a day. The metallic clinging sound stayed in my ears and above my head. I did not complain much then, but the noise was there, pressing on the night while I tried to rest inside Toyotaki.

在那之前，我曾经在 parking app 里看到有人留言提到 GoSwift 系统。可是我看不明白，所以就忽略了。

Before that, I had seen one comment in the parking app mentioning the GoSwift system at the border. I could not understand what it meant, so I ignored it.

当天我去 Luhamaa border 排队。排了三个小时之后，Estonia officer 才注意到我，并告诉我需要先到附近的 counter 取得一个 ticket，就像银行排队号码那样。

That day, I queued at the Luhamaa border for three hours. Only then did an Estonian officer notice me and tell me that I first needed to go to a nearby counter to obtain a ticket, something like the queue number ticket used in a bank.

我去了 counter。最快的下一个 slot 是三天后。

I went to the counter. The fastest available slot was three days later.

Bang。没有选择。

Bang. No choice.

我付了额外费用，在 Luhamaa Border Parking 过夜。虽然 slot 是三天后，但我不敢开去 Estonia 其他地方，因为我怕错过或失去这个 ticket slot。

I paid extra and stayed overnight at Luhamaa Border Parking. Even though the slot was three days away, I did not dare to drive somewhere else in Estonia because I was afraid of losing the ticket slot.

6 月 9 日到 10 日，我决定移到 Vastseliina Metskond Camping。那里很安静，也很漂亮。Toyotaki 停在绿色草地和树林前面。等待没有消失，但至少那两天，等待有了一个比较温柔的地方。

From 9 to 10 June, I moved to Vastseliina Metskond Camping. It was quiet and beautiful. Toyotaki was parked beside the green field and forest. The waiting did not disappear, but for those two days, at least the waiting had a gentler place to stay.

6 月 13 日上午 11:37，我终于回到 Estonia Luhamaa border 排队。那一刻我其实是开心的。就像上一次在 Iran 边境等 Armenia e-visa 时一样，虽然还是在等待，可是“终于能排队”代表事情开始往前走。

On 13 June, at 11:37 a.m., I finally returned to queue at the Estonia Luhamaa border. I was actually happy. It was like the Armenia e-visa situation at the Iranian border: even though I was still waiting, being allowed to queue meant that something was finally moving forward.

上一次在 Iran 边境，我也曾经卡了四天，等 Armenia 的 e-visa。那种等待不是风景照里看得到的东西，可是它会留在身体里。

At the Iranian border, I had also been stuck for four days waiting for the Armenia e-visa. That kind of waiting is not something visible in scenic photographs, but it remains in the body.

Russia border 的检查非常严格，也非常仔细。每一辆车都会被详细检查。我心里还有另一个压力：海关只允许 3L 酒类，可是我一路经过欧洲各国时，都买了不同大小的 vodka，像是给那段路留下小小的纪念。

The Russian border was strict and detailed. Every vehicle was checked carefully. I also carried one private worry: customs only allowed 3 litres of alcohol, but along the way through Europe I had bought vodka bottles of different sizes, like small souvenirs from the countries I had passed.

6 月 13 日进入 Estonia Luhamaa border，6 月 14 日早上 6:56，我终于从 Russian APP Sumilkino border 出来。那一刻，我是真的很开心。

I entered the Estonia Luhamaa border on 13 June. On 14 June, at 6:56 a.m., I finally came out from the Russian APP Sumilkino border. At that moment, I was truly happy.

这不是王子公主式的旅行。它有天空很蓝的时候，也有冷冻货柜的噪音；有漂亮的 camping，也有看不懂的系统；有排队、有票、有海关、有担心，也有最后终于出来时的快乐。

This was not a prince-and-princess kind of journey. It had bright blue skies, but also the noise of a cold-storage container. It had beautiful camping, but also systems I did not understand. It had queues, tickets, customs, worry, and finally the happiness of making it through.

这就是路上的小系统：不戏剧化，也不一定好看，可是很有力量。一个看不懂的系统，可以把一次过境变成三天等待。一个 parking lot 可以变成卧室。一张票可以变成边界。一个噪音，也会成为记忆的一部分。

This is one of the small systems of the road: not dramatic, not always beautiful, but powerful. A system you do not understand can turn a border crossing into three days of waiting. A parking lot can become a bedroom. A ticket can become a border. A noise can become part of the memory.
