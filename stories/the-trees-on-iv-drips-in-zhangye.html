The Trees on IV Drips in Zhangye

张掖打点滴的树

从敦煌的万人星空演唱会，到嘉峪关，再到张掖，路上的记忆突然从大场面变成一个很小的细节：树在打点滴。

From Dunhuang’s desert karaoke night to Jiayuguan, and then to Zhangye, the memory of the road suddenly shifted from a huge scene to a tiny detail: trees on IV drips.

Location
Zhangye / China
Route Node
China / Small Road Observation and Hotel Night
Date
25 July 2024

在张掖，我看到树旁边挂着像点滴一样的袋子和管子。那个画面对我来说很特别，因为它不像景点，也不像打卡照。它只是中国路边一个很小、很具体的照顾方式。

In Zhangye, I saw bags and tubes hanging beside trees, like IV drips. The scene felt special to me because it was not a tourist spot, and not a checklist photo. It was simply a small, concrete way of caring for street trees in China.

这种小东西很适合放进 archive。因为真正的长途旅行，不只是国家、边境和大景点，也包括那些一闪而过、以后却会突然想起的路边细节。

This kind of small detail belongs in the archive. A long overland journey is not only countries, borders, and famous places. It is also made of tiny roadside details that pass quickly, but return to memory later.

25 July 的 sleeping area 是瑞纳酒店，张掖，中国。Toyotaki 停在酒店外，继续成为我的房子、我的仓库，也是这条路移动中的身体。

The sleeping area on 25 July was Ruina Hotel in Zhangye, China. Toyotaki parked outside the hotel, still serving as my house, my storage space, and the moving body of this journey.

这一篇先作为 skeleton anchor 留下来。以后当更多硬盘里的照片和视频回来，这些短短的节点会变成可以继续插入记忆的地方。

This memory remains here first as a skeleton anchor. Later, when more photos and videos return from the hard drives, these short nodes will become places where more memories can be inserted.
