China · Xi’an ↔ Hong Kong · 29–30 July 2024

The Tunnel, the Flight, and the Same-Day Exit

麦积山隧道、航班，和同日折返

29 July 的路线，不只是去机场。它先经过麦积山隧道——12.29 km 长，真的不是开玩笑。然后，路从地面变成航班，从 Toyotaki 变成飞机，从 team road 变成一个人的行政折返。

The route on 29 July was not only about going to the airport. It first passed through Maiji Mountain Tunnel — 12.29 km long, truly not a joke. Then the road changed from ground to flight, from Toyotaki to an airplane, from team road into a solo administrative turn.

Location
Maiji Mountain Tunnel → Xi’an → Hong Kong → Xi’an / China
Route Node
China / 15-Day Visa-Free Same-Day Exit and Return
Date
29–30 July 2024

麦积山隧道很长。12.29 km 的 tunnel，对 Toyotaki 来说像是一段被山吞进去的路。中国段常常有这种尺度感：桥很长、隧道很长、路很大，车辆也很密。

Maiji Mountain Tunnel was long. A 12.29 km tunnel felt like a section of road swallowed by the mountain. The China section often carried this sense of scale: long bridges, long tunnels, large roads, and dense traffic.

这一天的重点，是 15-day visa-free 的 same-day exit and return。我的航班安排是：29 July 10:35am 从西安飞 Hong Kong，17:50 从 Hong Kong 回来。

The key task that day was the 15-day visa-free same-day exit and return. My flight plan was: 29 July, 10:35 a.m. from Xi’an to Hong Kong, and 17:50 from Hong Kong back.

这是很奇怪的一种旅行：不是为了玩 Hong Kong，而是为了让中国路段可以继续。前一天还在杜甫草堂和边方村停车场，接下来就要把自己从中国飞出去，再同一天飞回来。

It was a strange kind of travel: not to visit Hong Kong, but to allow the China route to continue. The day before, I was still at Du Fu Thatched Cottage and Bianfang Village Parking Area; now I had to fly myself out of China and return the same day.

第一次上了飞机，机长却说要在机内等多一个小时。那种感觉有点哭笑不得：visa clock 已经很紧了，人已经坐进飞机里，结果还要继续等。

After boarding the plane, the captain announced that we had to wait inside the aircraft for another hour. It was both funny and stressful: the visa clock was already tight, I was already inside the plane, and still I had to keep waiting.

30 July 的 sleeping area，是云朵酒店，西安，中国。回到西安以后，这个 hotel night 像一个回收点：我完成了同日折返，再回到 China route 里面。

The sleeping area on 30 July was Yunduo Hotel in Xi’an, China. After returning to Xi’an, this hotel night felt like a recovery point: I had completed the same-day exit and returned into the China route.

所以这篇不是 aviation story，也不是 Hong Kong travel story。它是 China route 里面的一条行政支线：隧道、机场、飞机延误、same-day exit，再回到 Toyotaki 和中国路上。

So this is not an aviation story, and not a Hong Kong travel story. It is an administrative branch inside the China route: tunnel, airport, airplane delay, same-day exit, and then returning to Toyotaki and the China road.
