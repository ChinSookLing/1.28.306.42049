Thailand · Hat Yai · 22–24 August 2024

The Welcome Banner in Hat Yai

合艾的欢迎布条

这里还不是 Malaysia，但已经像回家的开始。经过 Laos、Thailand 的 border rules、雨夜、camping ground、海边 sleeping area，Hat Yai 成了这条路第一次真正遇见家人的地方。

This was not yet Malaysia, but it already felt like the beginning of home. After Laos and Thailand — border rules, rainy nights, camping grounds, and sea-edge sleeping areas — Hat Yai became the first place where the road truly met family.

Location
Old Mayflower Grande Hotel / Hat Yai / Thailand
Route Node
Thailand / Family Reunion and Temple Thanksgiving
Date
22–24 August 2024

终于在 Thailand Hat Yai 见到家人。最先落进记忆里的，不是一个景点，而是妈妈的手。走在合艾夜晚的街上，牵着她，路好像忽然从一个人的 overland，变回一家人的生活。

I finally met my family in Hat Yai, Thailand. The first thing that entered the memory was not a tourist sight, but my mother’s hand. Walking through Hat Yai at night, holding her hand, the road suddenly changed from a solo overland journey back into family life.

家人还准备了 welcome banner。那不是普通的布条。它把一路走过的国家、路线、担心、等待和欢迎，全都摊开在 Toyotaki 前面。那一刻，路不只是自己记得，也被家人看见了。

My family had prepared a welcome banner. It was not just a banner. It spread the countries, the route, the worry, the waiting, and the welcome in front of Toyotaki. In that moment, the road was not only remembered by me — it was witnessed by family.

接着，我们去了附近有名的庙。不是为了观光，而是为了感谢一路平安抵达。经过那么长的路、那么多 border、repair、雨夜和 unknown sleeping area，能够带着 Toyotaki 和自己平安走到这里，本身就值得认真说一声谢谢。

Then we visited nearby well-known temples. This was not for sightseeing, but to give thanks for arriving safely. After such a long road, so many borders, repairs, rainy nights, and unknown sleeping areas, reaching this point safely with Toyotaki was something worth giving thanks for.

24 August 早上，我们又去了更多庙。家人不是只来接我，也像是在帮这条路收尾：把平安抵达这件事，再一次带到庙里认真感谢。

On the morning of 24 August, we visited more temples. My family was not only there to meet me; it felt as if they were helping the road close properly, bringing the safe arrival into the temple again with gratitude.

哥哥说，这里是很好展示 welcome banner 的地方。于是我们又把布条拉出来。前一天它在 Toyotaki 前面欢迎我；这一天，它在大佛前面，把这段 29-country road 展开给天空和家人看。

My brother said this was a good place to show the welcome banner. So we pulled it out again. The day before, it had welcomed me in front of Toyotaki; this time, in front of the Big Buddha, it opened the 29-country road to the sky and to family.

后来我们也到了 Khlong Hae Floating Market 一带，在路边留下 family photo。Hat Yai 的这几天，不是正式回到 Malaysia，却已经把“家人在等我”这件事变成了真实画面。

Later, we also reached the Khlong Hae Floating Market area and kept a roadside family photo. These days in Hat Yai were not yet the official return to Malaysia, but they had already turned “family was waiting for me” into a real image.

然后就是 Malaysian 最真实的 happiness：吃。Lunch、tea time、dinner，一餐接一餐。身为马来西亚人，吃，本来就是人生很大的享受。经过 Laos、Thailand 一路开回来，终于能和家人坐下来吃饭，那种感觉像是在庆祝自己真的平安走到了这里。

Then came the most Malaysian form of happiness: eating. Lunch, tea time, dinner — one meal after another. As a Malaysian, eating is one of life’s great enjoyments. After driving back through Laos and Thailand, being able to sit down with family for meals felt like a small celebration of having truly arrived safely.

这篇不是 Thailand sightseeing。它是 return arc 的 family reunion：还没有真正回到 Malaysia，但家已经先在 Hat Yai 出现了。

This is not a Thailand sightseeing story. It is the family reunion of the return arc: I had not yet truly returned to Malaysia, but home had already appeared in Hat Yai.
