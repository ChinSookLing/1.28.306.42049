Toyotaki Returned, Then the Ferry Began

Toyotaki 回来了，然后渡轮开始

5 July，Toyotaki 终于从 Georgia / Tbilisi 运到 Baku, Azerbaijan。运输费用是 USD 580。它不是和我一起过来的，而是被 transporter 带着走了另一条路。

On 5 July, Toyotaki finally reached Baku, Azerbaijan from Georgia / Tbilisi. The transport cost was USD 580. It did not arrive with me; it had travelled separately on a transporter.

Location
Baku Customs → Alat Port / Azerbaijan
Route Node
Azerbaijan · Toyotaki Return / Caspian Ferry Gate
Date
5 July 2024

到现场时，司机搞不清楚 Toyotaki 的 hand brake。结果最后不是别人帮我把车开下来，而是我自己上去，把 Toyotaki 从车上开下来。

At the unloading point, the driver could not figure out Toyotaki’s hand brake. In the end, nobody else drove it down for me — I climbed in myself and drove Toyotaki down from the transporter.

可是 Toyotaki 回到我身边，并不代表 Plan C 完成。下一道门不是陆路边境，而是 Caspian Sea：从 Alat, Azerbaijan 坐 ferry 到 Kuryk, Kazakhstan。

But Toyotaki returning to me did not mean Plan C was complete. The next gate was not a land border, but the Caspian Sea: a ferry from Alat, Azerbaijan to Kuryk, Kazakhstan.

这段 ferry 是 Zarifa Aliyeva，从 Alat 到 Kuryk。航程大约 30 个小时，ferry 费用 USD 560。公开版单据里，我只保留路线和费用记忆，姓名、证件、车牌、bill number 都隐藏。

The ferry was the Zarifa Aliyeva, travelling from Alat to Kuryk. The sea crossing took about 30 hours, and the ferry cost was USD 560. In the public version of the document, I kept only the route and cost memory; personal name, document details, plate number, and bill number are hidden.

那时候我的目标很简单：如果成功到达 Kuryk，就开启长奔 mode，用 8 到 10 天跑 3800 km，只求 15 July 可以顺利到达 Horgos。

At that time, my goal was simple: if I could reach Kuryk successfully, I would enter long-run mode — 3,800 km in 8 to 10 days, aiming to reach Horgos safely by 15 July.

这一篇 memory 不是风景，而是 logistics 的重量：USD 580 的陆路运输，USD 560 的 ferry，30 小时的海上航程，还有一个很清楚的目标——只要 Toyotaki 和我能到 Kuryk，后面就继续跑。

This memory is not about scenery. It is about the weight of logistics: USD 580 for land transport, USD 560 for the ferry, a 30-hour sea crossing, and one clear goal — if Toyotaki and I could reach Kuryk, we would keep going.
