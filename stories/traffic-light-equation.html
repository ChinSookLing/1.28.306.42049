Real Story

The day the world became too funny to obey traffic rules.

At a traffic light in Kolkata, the electric three-wheeler had already started to move. The driver looked back and laughed. The traffic policeman waved like the whole road depended on his arms. The fruit seller remained calm, slowly placing fruit as if the universe had not entered panic mode.

Tuzi was in the middle of it all: holding fruit, finding coins, trying to understand whether to wait, pay, move, laugh, or survive the moment.

Why This Memory Stayed

Because the oranges became two tiny suns.

Twenty years later, the exact traffic light, plate number, and street timing may disappear. But the oranges remain. They are not just fruit. They are the proof that the day was real, ridiculous, bright, and impossible to forget.

Kolkata before the road · Uber, Misti Hub, Eco Park, and the Orange Theorem

26/10/2023 was still before driving. Toyotaki had not been released yet, so Tuzi moved around Kolkata by Uber, connected to her Visa card. In Malaysia, Grab felt more familiar; in India, Uber became the practical way to move while waiting for Toyotaki.

这一天还不是 driving day。Toyotaki 还没有 release，所以 Tuzi 用 Uber 在 Kolkata 移动，而且 Uber 连接 Visa card，很方便。在 Malaysia 比较自然想到 Grab；但在 India，这一天是 Uber 带我去看 Kolkata。

At Misti Hub, Kolkata appeared through sweets. Ms Priyanka Das introduced the variety of local sweets, and through her, the city was not only traffic and waiting; it became taste, hospitality, and local pride.

在 Misti Hub，Kolkata 变成了甜点的城市。Ms Priyanka Das 向我介绍各种 local sweets。通过她，Kolkata 不只是交通、等待和喇叭声，也变成味道、热情和 local pride。
