India · Kolkata · Story 12

Welcome to India

I was simply walking and filming the streets of Kolkata. Then some local ladies came over.

India Kolkata People Welcome Street Life

I was walking through Kolkata with my camera, filming the street and whatever caught my attention.

Then a few local ladies noticed me. Instead of simply walking past, they came over and started talking to me.

They were kind and curious. So I decided to make a little video with them.

I guided them one by one to say their names out loud. It was simple, spontaneous and a little funny.

And then, at the end, all of them said together:

“Welcome to India!”

That was it.

No famous landmark. No planned tour. Just a few kind people who saw a traveller walking with a camera and decided to say hello.

I think these are the moments that make travelling memorable.
