Georgia → Azerbaijan · 29 June–2 July 2024

While Toyotaki Travelled Separately

Toyotaki 先走，我飞去 Baku

Plan C 真正开始的时候，路被分成两条：Toyotaki 被 transporter 送去 Azerbaijan 的 Baku，我自己则临时飞过去接它。

When Plan C truly began, the road split into two lines: Toyotaki travelled separately by transporter toward Baku, Azerbaijan, while I flew ahead to meet it there.

Location
Georgia → Tbilisi Airport → Baku / Azerbaijan
Route Node
Azerbaijan · Plan C / Separated Vehicle Route
Date
29 June–2 July 2024

29 June，搬运公司告诉我：Toyotaki 已经 upload 上车，开始往 Azerbaijan 去了。一切看起来很顺利。那一刻，我知道 Plan C 已经不是纸上计划，而是真的开始动了。

On 29 June, the transport company told me that Toyotaki had already been loaded onto the carrier and was on the way to Azerbaijan. Everything looked smooth. At that moment, Plan C was no longer only a plan on paper; it had started moving.

可是 1 July 晚上大约 11:30pm，我突然发现自己还需要 Azerbaijan e-visa。那一刻真的吓到：明天就要飞了，visa 还没申请。

But around 11:30 p.m. on 1 July, I suddenly realised that I still needed an Azerbaijan e-visa. It was frightening: I was supposed to fly the next day, but I had not even applied for the visa yet.

很幸运，我竟然在大约 5 分钟内拿到 e-visa。那种感觉是：OMG，路又差一点卡住，可是又刚刚好开了一条缝。

Luckily, I managed to obtain the e-visa in about five minutes. It felt like: OMG, the road almost got blocked again, but a small opening appeared just in time.

到了 Baku 以后，我住在 Maajid Hotel。那一晚不是旅行式的轻松，而是 Plan C 的第一晚：我已经到了 Azerbaijan，但 Toyotaki 还在它自己的路上。

After arriving in Baku, I stayed at Maajid Hotel. That night did not feel like an easy tourist night. It was the first night of Plan C: I had arrived in Azerbaijan, but Toyotaki was still on its own route.

这篇 memory 记录的是一种很特别的分离：车在路上，人先飞到前方。不是舒服的安排，而是为了让整段旅程继续下去。

This memory records a very particular kind of separation: the vehicle stayed on the road, while I flew ahead. It was not a comfortable arrangement; it was what had to happen so the journey could continue.
