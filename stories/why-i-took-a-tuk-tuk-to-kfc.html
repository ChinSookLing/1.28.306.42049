Why I Took a Tuk-Tuk to KFC

Kolkata could be painful, chaotic, funny and fast — and Tuzi decided to experience it from inside a Tuk-Tuk.

Kolkata Tuk-Tuk Street Life KFC India

Archive type: Real Memory

Status: Photo + Video Memory

The Other Side of Kolkata

Sometimes a photograph makes you stop.

On the roadside, Tuzi saw someone sleeping outside. Behind him was a police car.

It was one of those moments that was difficult to explain. The city continued moving, but this small scene stayed there.

India was not only beautiful places and interesting food. There were also scenes that were uncomfortable to look at.

Into the Traffic

Then came the Tuk-Tuk.

The road was busy, noisy and full of movement. From inside the Tuk-Tuk, the traffic felt completely different.

It sometimes felt as if the Tuk-Tuks were racing with everything else on the road.

And Why Did I Take It?

Because I wanted to try KFC in India. 😂

After all the traffic, noise and unfamiliar scenes, the reason was actually very simple.

Tuzi wanted to try KFC in India.

Maybe that is travel. You can see something painful in the morning, jump into a noisy Tuk-Tuk, watch the city race past you, and still end up chasing something as ordinary as a KFC.

The contradiction is part of the memory.
